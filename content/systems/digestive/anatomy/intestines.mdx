The intestines are a 7–8 meter tube of muscular conduit + absorptive epithelium. The **small intestine** (~6 m: duodenum, jejunum, ileum) does most digestion + nutrient absorption. The **large intestine** (~1.5 m: cecum, colon, rectum) reclaims water + electrolytes + houses the microbiome.

## Small intestine - three segments

| Segment | Length | Location | Distinguishing features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Duodenum** | 25 cm | Retroperitoneal (except first part); C-shape around pancreas head | Brunner glands (alkaline mucus); receives bile + pancreatic juice at ampulla of Vater (D2) |
| **Jejunum** | 2.5 m | Intraperitoneal; LUQ; long vasa recta + few arcades | Plicae circulares (tallest), most villi, primary absorption site |
| **Ileum** | 3.5 m | Intraperitoneal; RLQ; short vasa recta + many arcades; **Peyer patches** | Terminal ileum: B12 + bile salt reabsorption; junction with cecum at ileocecal valve |

### Microscopic adaptations for absorption (~250 m² surface area)
| Level | Magnification |
|---|---|
| **Plicae circulares** (Kerckring folds) | 3× |
| **Villi** | 10× |
| **Microvilli** (brush border) | 20× |

**Total** ~600× the surface of a smooth tube.

### Enterocytes + crypt cells
| Cell | Location | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Enterocyte** | Villus | Absorptive; brush border with disaccharidases (lactase, sucrase, maltase) + transporters (SGLT1, GLUT5, PepT1) |
| **Goblet cell** | Villus + crypt | Mucus secretion |
| **Paneth cell** | Crypt base | Defensins + lysozyme - antimicrobial defense |
| **Enteroendocrine cells** | Villus + crypt | Hormones - see GI hormones below |
| **M cell** | Over Peyer patches (ileum) | Sample antigen + present to underlying lymphocytes |
| **Stem cells** | Crypt | Replace epithelium every 3–5 days |

## Three regions, three blood supplies (recap)

| Region | Embryologic gut | Artery | Pain referral |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foregut** (stomach → 2nd duodenum) | - | Celiac trunk (T12) | Epigastric |
| **Midgut** (3rd duodenum → proximal 2/3 transverse colon) | - | SMA (L1) | Periumbilical |
| **Hindgut** (distal 1/3 transverse colon → upper rectum) | - | IMA (L3) | Suprapubic |

Anal canal below the pectinate line: somatic - internal pudendal artery + nerve.

## Large intestine - cecum to anal canal

<Figure name="digestive/colon" caption="Large intestine with each segment in order: cecum + appendix, ascending colon, transverse colon, descending colon, sigmoid colon, rectum, and anus." />

| Segment | Location | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Cecum** | RLQ | Blind pouch; appendix attaches at posteromedial wall |
| **Appendix** | RLQ (variable) | 6–10 cm; lymphoid tissue; tip can be retrocecal (most common, 65%) |
| **Ascending colon** | Right side | Retroperitoneal (secondary) |
| **Hepatic flexure** | Right upper | Junction to transverse |
| **Transverse colon** | Anterior | Intraperitoneal; transverse mesocolon |
| **Splenic flexure** | LUQ | "Watershed" - junction of SMA + IMA territories |
| **Descending colon** | Left side | Retroperitoneal |
| **Sigmoid colon** | LLQ | Intraperitoneal; sigmoid mesocolon |
| **Rectum** | Pelvis | No mesentery; transverse rectal folds |
| **Anal canal** | Pelvic floor | Pectinate line divides upper (endoderm, visceral innervation, hindgut) from lower (ectoderm, somatic innervation) |

### Distinguishing features of the colon
| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Teniae coli** | 3 longitudinal smooth muscle bands (incomplete outer layer) |
| **Haustra** | Outpouchings between teniae - sacculations |
| **Epiploic appendages** | Fat-filled peritoneal pouches |
| **Plicae semilunares** | Internal folds between haustra |

Small intestine has none of these - diagnostic landmark on imaging.

### Watershed zones (vascular)
- **Splenic flexure** - between SMA + IMA → susceptible to ischemic colitis (#1 site).
- **Rectosigmoid junction** - between IMA + superior rectal vs middle/inferior rectal.

In hypotension, watershed areas suffer first.

<Clinical>
**Appendicitis** - luminal obstruction (fecalith) → infection → necrosis → perforation. Classic: periumbilical pain → localizes to RLQ over hours (McBurney point, 2/3 from umbilicus to ASIS); fever; ↑ WBC. Signs: Rovsing (LLQ palpation → RLQ pain), Psoas (extension of right hip → pain), Obturator (internal rotation of flexed thigh → pain).

**Diverticulosis** vs **diverticulitis** - outpouchings (false diverticula - only mucosa + submucosa, herniating through muscle at the vasa recta entry) most commonly in **sigmoid colon**. Diverticulosis = painless bleeding; diverticulitis = LLQ pain + fever + leukocytosis from microperforation.

**Volvulus**: twisting of bowel on its mesentery. **Sigmoid volvulus** = elderly, chronic constipation; "**coffee bean**" sign on X-ray. **Cecal volvulus** = younger; rotation around long mesentery.

**Ischemic colitis** - splenic flexure (watershed); elderly with cardiovascular disease + acute LLQ pain + bloody diarrhea. CT shows "thumbprinting." Most resolve with bowel rest; surgery if peritonitis.
</Clinical>

## GI hormones - board favorites

| Hormone | Source | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gastrin** | G cells (antrum + duodenum) | Vagal stimulation, peptides | ↑ HCl from parietal cells; trophic effect on gastric mucosa |
| **Secretin** | S cells (duodenum) | ↑ H⁺ + fatty acids in duodenum | ↑ pancreatic HCO₃⁻ + bile flow; ↓ gastric acid |
| **CCK** | I cells (duodenum + jejunum) | Fatty acids + amino acids | Gallbladder contraction; sphincter of Oddi relaxation; pancreatic enzyme secretion; satiety |
| **GIP** | K cells (duodenum + jejunum) | Glucose, AA, fats | ↑ insulin (incretin); ↓ gastric acid |
| **GLP-1** | L cells (ileum + colon) | Nutrients | ↑ insulin (incretin); ↓ glucagon; ↓ gastric emptying; satiety |
| **Motilin** | M cells (small intestine) | Fasting | Migrating motor complexes (MMC) every 90 min - clears residue between meals |
| **Somatostatin** | δ cells (pancreas + GI) | ↓ pH, fat, AA | Pan-inhibitor (insulin, glucagon, gastrin, secretin, CCK) - "global brake" |
| **VIP** | Parasympathetic enteric ganglia | Distension | ↑ intestinal water + electrolyte secretion (↓ acid, ↑ relaxation) |

**Octreotide** = synthetic somatostatin → treats variceal bleeding (↓ portal blood flow), carcinoid syndrome (↓ serotonin secretion), VIPoma, acromegaly.

## Absorption sites - what comes in where

| Nutrient | Where | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron** | Duodenum | DMT1 (Fe²⁺); vitamin C aids reduction of Fe³⁺ |
| **Folate** | Duodenum + jejunum | Reduced + methylated; carrier protein |
| **Calcium** | Duodenum + jejunum | Active (vitamin D-dependent) + passive |
| **Glucose + galactose** | Jejunum | SGLT1 (Na-coupled) apical; GLUT2 basolateral |
| **Fructose** | Jejunum | GLUT5 apical; GLUT2 basolateral (facilitated) |
| **Amino acids** | Jejunum | Multiple Na-coupled transporters |
| **Fats** | Jejunum + proximal ileum | Bile salt micelles → diffuse into enterocyte → reassembled to TGs → chylomicrons → lymph (lacteals) |
| **Bile salts** | **Terminal ileum** | Active (apical Na/bile salt cotransporter) |
| **Vitamin B12** | **Terminal ileum** | Bound to **intrinsic factor** (from gastric parietal cells); receptor: cubilin |
| **Water + electrolytes** | Mostly colon | ENaC + aldosterone-regulated; CFTR for chloride |
| **Short-chain fatty acids** | Colon | From microbiome fermentation of dietary fiber; energy source for colonocytes (butyrate) |

<Clinical>
**Celiac disease** - autoimmune reaction to gluten (gliadin); HLA-DQ2/DQ8. **Villous atrophy** + crypt hyperplasia + intraepithelial lymphocytes → **malabsorption** + steatorrhea + iron deficiency + osteoporosis + dermatitis herpetiformis. Serology: anti-tissue transglutaminase IgA + anti-endomysial. Treat: lifelong gluten-free diet.

**Crohn disease**: transmural, **skip lesions**, anywhere mouth-to-anus (terminal ileum + ascending colon most common), cobblestoning, fistulas, granulomas. Smoking worsens. Treat: anti-TNF biologics + steroids.

**Ulcerative colitis**: mucosal only, **continuous from rectum proximally**, never beyond colon. Bloody diarrhea + tenesmus. Smoking is protective (paradox). Risk: **PSC** + colon cancer. Curable by total colectomy.

**Lactose intolerance**: lactase deficiency → undigested lactose → osmotic diarrhea + flatulence; common in adults of non-European descent.
</Clinical>

## Function reference - every component

| Component | Function | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Brunner glands** | Alkaline mucus in duodenum | Neutralize gastric acid; hypertrophy in PUD |
| **Plicae circulares + villi + microvilli** | ↑ absorptive surface area 600× | Lost in celiac (villous atrophy) |
| **Crypts of Lieberkühn** | Stem cell zone + Paneth cells | Renew epithelium q3–5 days; defensins |
| **Peyer patches** | Mucosa-associated lymphoid tissue in ileum | Site of immune sampling; involved in typhoid + Crohn |
| **Ileocecal valve** | One-way control between SI + LI | Obstruction → distension proximal; in cecal volvulus, often spares small bowel |
| **Appendix** | Lymphoid + microbial reservoir | Inflammation = appendicitis; not essential |
| **Teniae coli** | Longitudinal muscle | Pathognomonic for colon on imaging |
| **Vasa recta of colon** | Penetrate through muscle layer | Weak points → diverticula form here |
| **Cecal pole** | Largest diameter; thinnest wall | Most likely to perforate in distal obstruction (Laplace) |
| **Pectinate line** | Embryologic + clinical watershed of anal canal | Above: visceral pain, lymph to internal iliac; Below: somatic pain, lymph to superficial inguinal |
| **Internal anal sphincter** | Smooth muscle, involuntary | Relaxes with rectal distension (rectoanal inhibitory reflex) |
| **External anal sphincter** | Skeletal, voluntary, pudendal n. | Voluntary continence |
| **Migrating motor complex (motilin)** | Cleans residual chyme between meals | Stimulated by erythromycin (motilin agonist) - pre-op gastric emptying |

## Why this matters

<HighYield variant="boards">
- **Carcinoid syndrome** - neuroendocrine tumor (typically small bowel) → serotonin → flushing, diarrhea, wheezing, right heart valve fibrosis (tricuspid). Symptoms appear when liver is bypassed (metastases). Treat: octreotide.
- **Zollinger-Ellison syndrome** - gastrinoma (often duodenal) → recurrent peptic ulcers + diarrhea + steatorrhea. Associated with MEN1. ↑ gastrin even after secretin (paradoxical).
- **Colorectal cancer screen**: colonoscopy at 45 → every 10 years (or earlier with family history, IBD, FAP, Lynch).
- **FAP** (familial adenomatous polyposis): APC mutation; thousands of polyps; 100% cancer risk by 40 → prophylactic colectomy. Variants: Gardner (+ osteomas, desmoids), Turcot (+ CNS tumors).
- **Lynch / HNPCC**: mismatch repair gene mutations (MLH1, MSH2). Right-sided tumors + endometrial + ovarian.
- **B12 deficiency**: terminal ileum disease (Crohn, resection), pernicious anemia (autoimmune anti-IF), strict vegan, metformin (long-term). Megaloblastic anemia + neurological (subacute combined degeneration).
- **Iron deficiency in older adult or postmenopausal woman**: workup for **occult GI malignancy** (colonoscopy + EGD).
- **C. diff colitis** - post-antibiotic (clindamycin, fluoroquinolones, cephalosporins). Watery diarrhea + abdominal pain + ↑ WBC. **Pseudomembranes** on colonoscopy. Treat: oral vancomycin or fidaxomicin.
- **Volvulus** common sites: sigmoid (elderly, mental institutions) > cecal (young, mobile cecum).
- **Hirschsprung disease**: absence of ganglion cells in distal colon → no peristalsis → functional obstruction. Failure to pass meconium in 48 h; explosive expulsion on rectal exam. Biopsy diagnostic.
</HighYield>

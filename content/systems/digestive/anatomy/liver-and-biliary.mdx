The liver is the largest internal organ (~1.5 kg) and the chemical factory of the body - synthesizing proteins, processing nutrients, conjugating bilirubin, detoxifying drugs, and storing iron + glycogen + vitamins. It is also the central organ of the **portal circulation**: every drop of blood from the GI tract passes through it before reaching systemic circulation (**first-pass metabolism**).

When studying liver disease, follow two flows separately: **blood comes in through the portal vein and hepatic artery**, while **bile drains out in the opposite direction through canaliculi and ducts**. Many questions become easier once those two directions are kept distinct.

## Liver - gross anatomy

- **Location**: RUQ, mostly intraperitoneal except for the "**bare area**" on the diaphragm (no peritoneum).
- **Four anatomic lobes**: right (largest), left, **caudate** (posterior, on IVC), **quadrate** (anterior to porta hepatis).
- **Functional Couinaud segments**: 8 (numbered I–VIII clockwise) defined by portal pedicles + hepatic veins - basis for **segmentectomies + transplant**.

### Peritoneal attachments
| Ligament | What | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Falciform** | Sickle-shaped reflection between liver + anterior abdominal wall | **Ligamentum teres hepatis** (remnant of left umbilical vein); patent in portal HTN → caput medusae |
| **Coronary** | Reflects liver to diaphragm | Encloses bare area |
| **Triangular** | Right + left lateral extensions of coronary | - |
| **Lesser omentum** | Liver → lesser curvature of stomach | Hepatogastric + hepatoduodenal (contains **portal triad**) |

### Porta hepatis (hepatoduodenal ligament contents)
- **Common bile duct** (right) - anterior right
- **Hepatic artery proper** (left) - anterior left
- **Portal vein** - posterior (behind both)

<Mnemonic>
**"DUCK"** at the porta: **D**uct (right), **A**rtery (left), **P**ortal vein (back). Or the surgical mnemonic - the **Pringle maneuver** = compressing the hepatoduodenal ligament between thumb + index finger to control hepatic bleeding (occludes portal + arterial inflow).
</Mnemonic>

## Liver microanatomy - the lobule + acinus

Two complementary structural concepts:

### Classic hexagonal lobule
- Central **central vein** (drains to hepatic vein → IVC).
- Six **portal triads** at the corners - bile duct + portal venule + hepatic arteriole.
- **Sinusoids** = leaky capillaries lined by fenestrated endothelium + **Kupffer cells** (resident macrophages).
- Blood flows from portal triad → sinusoids → central vein.

### Acinus (functional - for understanding zonal injury)
- Zone 1 (periportal) - most oxygen, first to receive nutrients/toxins; least vulnerable to ischemia.
- Zone 2 (midzonal)
- Zone 3 (pericentral, around central vein) - **least oxygen, most vulnerable to ischemia + drug toxicity** (acetaminophen, alcoholic hepatitis, ischemic hepatitis); contains most cytochrome P450 → site of drug bioactivation.

### Cell types
| Cell | Function |
|---|---|
| **Hepatocyte** | Bulk of liver mass; ~80% of cells; do all the major synthetic + detox work |
| **Cholangiocyte** | Lines bile ducts; secretes bicarbonate-rich fluid |
| **Kupffer cell** | Sinusoidal macrophage; clears bacteria from portal blood + old RBCs |
| **Hepatic stellate (Ito) cell** | Space of Disse; stores vitamin A; activates to myofibroblast → fibrosis (cirrhosis) |
| **Sinusoidal endothelial cell** | Fenestrated, no basement membrane → free exchange |

## Bile + the biliary tree

### Composition of bile
- Water (97%) + **bile salts** (conjugated cholic + chenodeoxycholic acid) + **phospholipids** (lecithin) + **cholesterol** + **bilirubin glucuronide** + electrolytes.
- Bile salts have **detergent action** - emulsify fats for absorption.
- ~95% of bile salts are reabsorbed in the **terminal ileum** (enterohepatic circulation) - recycled 5–10× per day.

### Path of bile
```
Hepatocytes
   ↓
Bile canaliculi (between hepatocytes)
   ↓
Bile ductules → interlobular bile ducts
   ↓
Right + Left hepatic ducts
   ↓ (merge at porta hepatis)
Common hepatic duct
   ↓ (joined by cystic duct from gallbladder)
Common bile duct (CBD)
   ↓ (joined by pancreatic duct)
Ampulla of Vater
   ↓ (sphincter of Oddi)
Second part of duodenum (major duodenal papilla)
```

### Gallbladder
- **Location**: fossa on undersurface of liver, between right + quadrate lobes.
- **Three parts**: fundus (palpable at 9th costal cartilage / right midclavicular line), body, neck.
- **Hartmann's pouch** = pouch at neck where gallstones lodge.
- **Function**: concentrates bile (5–20×) + stores between meals.
- **Triggered by CCK** (from I cells of duodenum) → contraction + sphincter of Oddi relaxation.

### Triangle of Calot
- Boundaries: **cystic duct** (lateral), **common hepatic duct** (medial), **inferior liver edge** (superior).
- Contains: **cystic artery** (from right hepatic in 80%).
- Surgical landmark in cholecystectomy - must identify before clipping.

<Clinical>
**Gallstones** - 70% cholesterol (radiolucent), 30% pigment (radiopaque; black = hemolysis, brown = infection). Risk: **5 F's** - Female, Fat, Forty, Fertile, Family history.

**Cholecystitis**: stone impacted in cystic duct → gallbladder inflammation. **Murphy sign** = arrest of inspiration with RUQ palpation. RUQ pain + fever + leukocytosis. Ultrasound: thick wall, pericholecystic fluid, stones.

**Choledocholithiasis**: stone in CBD → jaundice + RUQ pain + acholic stools + dark urine.

**Ascending cholangitis**: CBD obstruction + infection. **Charcot triad** = fever + jaundice + RUQ pain. **Reynolds pentad** adds shock + AMS → emergency ERCP for decompression.

**Mirizzi syndrome**: gallstone in cystic duct compresses common hepatic duct → obstructive jaundice.
</Clinical>

## Blood supply - dual

| Source | % of liver blood flow | % of O₂ supply |
|---|---|---|
| **Hepatic artery proper** | 25% | 50% |
| **Portal vein** | 75% | 50% |

The portal vein carries **deoxygenated but nutrient-rich** blood from GI + spleen + pancreas. The hepatic artery delivers oxygenated blood. They mix in the sinusoids.

### Hepatic veins
Three (right + middle + left) drain directly into IVC just below the diaphragm. The **caudate lobe** drains separately into the IVC - that's why it can be spared in Budd-Chiari syndrome (hepatic vein thrombosis).

## Portal hypertension - when the liver blocks flow

Cirrhosis → fibrotic + nodular liver → ↑ resistance to portal flow → **portal hypertension** → opens **portosystemic anastomoses** to bypass the liver (see [mesenteries](/digestive/anatomy/mesenteries) page for the four major sites).

### Clinical consequences
- **Esophageal varices** (left gastric ↔ azygos): can hemorrhage massively
- **Caput medusae** (paraumbilical ↔ epigastric): dilated periumbilical veins
- **Hemorrhoids** (superior rectal ↔ middle/inferior rectal): internal hemorrhoids
- **Splenomegaly** + thrombocytopenia (sequestration)
- **Ascites** (↑ hydrostatic + ↓ oncotic from ↓ albumin synthesis)
- **Hepatic encephalopathy** (ammonia bypasses liver → crosses BBB)

## Liver functions - every job at a glance

| Category | Function | Test / clinical |
|---|---|---|
| **Protein synthesis** | Albumin, clotting factors (II, VII, IX, X, fibrinogen, protein C/S), C-reactive protein, transferrin | ↓ in cirrhosis → ↓ albumin, ↑ PT/INR |
| **Carb metabolism** | Glycogenesis, glycogenolysis, gluconeogenesis | Hypoglycemia in fulminant liver failure |
| **Lipid metabolism** | Cholesterol synthesis, lipoprotein assembly (VLDL), bile acid synthesis, ketogenesis | Fatty liver, gallstones |
| **Protein catabolism** | Urea cycle - convert NH₃ → urea | Hyperammonemia in liver failure → encephalopathy |
| **Bile production** | Conjugate + excrete bilirubin | Jaundice in dysfunction; conjugated vs unconjugated localizes the problem |
| **Detoxification** | Phase I (CYP450) + Phase II (glucuronidation, sulfation) | Drug interactions; CYP inducers/inhibitors |
| **Storage** | Iron (ferritin), copper, B12, folate, vitamins A/D/E/K, glycogen | Iron overload in hemochromatosis; Wilson dz (copper) |
| **Immune** | Kupffer cells - bacterial clearance from portal blood | Disrupted in cirrhosis → spontaneous bacterial peritonitis |
| **Hematopoiesis** | Major site in fetus (weeks 6–22); resumes if marrow fails | Extramedullary hematopoiesis in myelofibrosis |

## Bilirubin metabolism - board favorite

1. **Senescent RBCs** → splenic macrophages → heme → biliverdin → **unconjugated (indirect) bilirubin**.
2. **Unconjugated** is lipid-soluble + albumin-bound → travels to liver.
3. **Hepatocyte UGT1A1** conjugates bilirubin with glucuronic acid → **conjugated (direct) bilirubin** - water-soluble.
4. Conjugated bilirubin **secreted into bile** → small intestine → bacteria deconjugate + reduce → **urobilinogen**.
5. ~80% urobilinogen excreted in feces as **stercobilin** (brown stool color); ~20% reabsorbed → portal vein → most re-excreted; small fraction enters kidney → **urobilin** (urine yellow color).

### Causes of jaundice
| Pattern | Mechanism | Diseases |
|---|---|---|
| **Unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia** | ↑ production OR ↓ uptake/conjugation | Hemolysis, Gilbert (mild UGT1A1), Crigler-Najjar (severe UGT1A1), neonatal physiologic |
| **Conjugated hyperbilirubinemia (intrinsic liver / intrahepatic cholestasis)** | Hepatocellular damage | Viral hepatitis, alcoholic, drug-induced, autoimmune, PBC/PSC |
| **Conjugated hyperbilirubinemia (extrahepatic / obstructive)** | Blocked bile flow | Gallstones, pancreatic CA, cholangiocarcinoma, biliary atresia |

Direct bilirubin in urine = obstructive or hepatocellular. Pale stools + dark urine = obstructive.

## Function reference - every component

| Component | Function | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Hepatocyte** | All synthetic + detox work | Target of viral hepatitis, alcohol, drugs |
| **Bile canaliculus** | Initial bile collection | Cholestasis = canalicular dysfunction |
| **Kupffer cell** | Phagocytose bacteria from portal blood | Cleared poorly in cirrhosis → spontaneous bacterial peritonitis |
| **Stellate (Ito) cell** | Vitamin A storage; **activates to myofibroblast in chronic injury → fibrosis** | Central player in cirrhosis pathogenesis |
| **Space of Disse** | Between sinusoid + hepatocyte | Site of stellate cells; loss of fenestrations → "capillarization" in cirrhosis → ↓ exchange |
| **Caudate lobe** | Drains directly to IVC | Spared in Budd-Chiari (hepatic vein thrombosis) → caudate hypertrophy |
| **Bile salts** | Emulsify dietary lipids | Reabsorbed in terminal ileum; resection or Crohn → fat malabsorption + steatorrhea |
| **Sphincter of Oddi** | Controls bile + pancreatic juice flow into duodenum | Dysfunction: post-cholecystectomy pain; spasm in opioid use |
| **Gallbladder** | Bile concentration + storage | Cholecystectomy generally well-tolerated; bile flows continuously after |
| **CYP450 system (zone 3)** | Phase I drug metabolism | Inducers (rifampin, phenytoin, carbamazepine, St John's wort, chronic alcohol); inhibitors (-azole antifungals, macrolides, grapefruit, acute alcohol, cimetidine) |
| **UGT1A1** | Conjugates bilirubin | Gilbert (mild deficiency, benign); Crigler-Najjar (severe) |
| **Albumin** | Oncotic pressure + drug carrier | ↓ in chronic liver failure → ascites, edema |
| **Coagulation factors** | Synthesis | All except VIII made in liver; vitamin K dependent factors II, VII, IX, X + protein C/S |
| **Ammonia → urea conversion** | Detoxify protein metabolism | Failure → hyperammonemia → hepatic encephalopathy |
| **Hepatic stellate cells** | Vitamin A storage + fibrosis driver | Therapeutic target in cirrhosis |
| **Cholangiocytes** | Bile duct lining; secrete bicarbonate | Targeted in PBC (small ducts) + PSC (medium + large ducts) |

## Why this matters

<HighYield variant="boards">
- **Hepatic transaminases**: AST + ALT. **ALT > AST → viral hepatitis** (chronic > acute). **AST > ALT (>2:1)** = **alcoholic** ("**A S**cotch and **T**onic," AST higher). Massive elevation (>1000): viral, ischemic (shock liver), or acetaminophen.
- **Alkaline phosphatase ↑** with **GGT ↑** = hepatobiliary obstruction. ALP ↑ alone (with normal GGT) = bone source.
- **PT/INR is the best functional liver test** (factor VII has shortest half-life, ~4 h).
- **Acetaminophen toxicity** - depletes glutathione → NAPQI (toxic metabolite) accumulates → hepatocyte necrosis (zone 3). Antidote: **N-acetylcysteine** (replenishes glutathione; effective even >24 h after ingestion in fulminant cases).
- **Wilson disease**: ATP7B mutation → can't excrete copper into bile → liver + brain + cornea (Kayser-Fleischer rings) accumulation. Low ceruloplasmin, ↑ urinary copper, ↑ liver copper on biopsy. Penicillamine, zinc.
- **Hemochromatosis**: HFE mutation → ↑ duodenal iron absorption → liver, pancreas, heart, joints, skin, gonads. Bronze diabetes. Treat: phlebotomy.
- **Hepatitis B serology**: HBsAg → infection; anti-HBs → immunity (vaccine or recovered); HBeAg → high infectivity; anti-HBc IgM → acute, IgG → past or chronic.
- **Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)** screen: AFP + ultrasound every 6 months in cirrhosis or chronic Hep B carriers. Major risk factors: HBV, HCV, alcohol, NASH, aflatoxin, hereditary hemochromatosis.
- **Primary biliary cholangitis (PBC)**: middle-aged woman + pruritus + fatigue + ↑ ALP + **anti-mitochondrial antibody (AMA)**. Histology: granulomatous bile duct destruction.
- **Primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC)**: middle-aged man + **ulcerative colitis** + ↑ ALP + "**onion skin**" fibrosis around ducts + "beads on a string" on MRCP + ↑ p-ANCA.
- **Biliary atresia**: progressive jaundice in newborn after 2 weeks of life → conjugated hyperbilirubinemia + acholic stools. **Kasai procedure** before 60 days for best outcome.
</HighYield>

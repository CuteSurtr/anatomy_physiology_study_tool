The job: break ingested food into small enough molecules to cross the enterocyte membrane, then absorb them. **Most absorption happens in the small intestine**, which has ~250 m² of surface area thanks to plicae circulares, villi, and microvilli.

<Figure name="digestive/peristalsis" caption="Peristaltic waves move chyme distally throughout digestion + absorption." />

## Macronutrient digestion

<Figure name="digestive/digestive-detail" caption="GI tract digestion overview. Carbs digested by α-amylase + brush border disaccharidases. Proteins by pepsin (stomach) + pancreatic trypsin/chymotrypsin/elastase + brush border peptidases. Fats by bile + pancreatic lipase." />

| Nutrient | Begins | Final products | Main absorption site |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carbohydrate** | Salivary amylase (mouth) | Glucose, galactose, fructose | Duodenum/jejunum |
| **Protein** | Pepsin (stomach) | Amino acids, di-/tripeptides | Jejunum (mostly) |
| **Fat** | Lingual + gastric lipase (minor) | Monoglycerides + FFAs | Jejunum (via micelles → chylomicrons) |

The **brush border** of enterocytes carries the final hydrolytic enzymes: lactase, sucrase, maltase, dextrinase, peptidases.

## Absorption mechanisms (worth memorizing)

<Figure name="digestive/villi-microvilli" caption="Villi + microvilli amplify absorptive surface ~600×. Each enterocyte has dedicated apical transporters: SGLT1 (glucose+Na), GLUT5 (fructose), PepT1 (di/tripeptides), FATP (fatty acids)." />

- **Glucose / galactose**: **SGLT1** (Na⁺-coupled) into enterocyte → **GLUT2** out into blood.
- **Fructose**: **GLUT5** in → **GLUT2** out (no Na⁺ needed).
- **Amino acids**: multiple Na⁺-coupled transporters.
- **Fats**: passive diffusion of MGs + FFAs from micelles → re-esterified inside enterocyte → packaged into **chylomicrons** → exit via lymphatic **lacteals**, not blood.
- **B12**: bound to intrinsic factor → absorbed in terminal ileum via specific receptor.
- **Iron**: Fe²⁺ via DMT1; regulated by **hepcidin** (↑ in inflammation → traps iron in enterocytes/macrophages).

## Hormonal control

<Figure name="digestive/gi-hormones-summary" caption="GI hormones at a glance - gastrin → HCl; CCK → bile + pancreas; secretin → HCO3-; GIP + GLP-1 → insulin; motilin → MMC; ghrelin → appetite." />

| Hormone | Source | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gastrin | G cells (antrum) | Peptides, stomach distension | ↑ HCl |
| CCK | I cells (duodenum) | Fats, proteins | ↑ pancreatic enzymes, ↑ gallbladder contraction, ↓ gastric emptying |
| Secretin | S cells (duodenum) | Acid in duodenum | ↑ pancreatic HCO₃⁻ |
| GIP | K cells (duodenum) | Glucose, fats | ↑ insulin (incretin effect) |

<Clinical>
**Lactose intolerance**: deficiency of brush-border lactase → undigested lactose reaches colon → osmotic diarrhea + bacterial fermentation (gas, bloating). Most adults worldwide are lactase non-persistent - it's the default, not the deficiency.

**Celiac disease**: gluten triggers immune-mediated villous atrophy in the proximal small intestine → malabsorption (iron, folate, calcium). Diagnose with anti-tTG IgA + duodenal biopsy.
</Clinical>

## Every GI enzyme, hormone, and transporter - function reference

### Digestive enzymes

| Enzyme | Source | Substrate | Why crucial |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Salivary α-amylase (ptyalin)** | Salivary glands | Starch → maltose, oligosaccharides | Starts carb digestion in mouth |
| **Lingual lipase** | Tongue glands | Triglycerides → MG + FFA | Minor; significant in newborns |
| **Pepsin** (from pepsinogen) | Chief cells (stomach) | Proteins → smaller peptides | Activated by acid; autocatalytic |
| **Gastric lipase** | Chief cells | TG → MG + FFA | Minor fat digestion |
| **Pancreatic α-amylase** | Pancreas | Starch → maltose, etc. | Major carb-digesting enzyme |
| **Trypsin** (from trypsinogen) | Pancreas | Proteins → peptides; activates other zymogens | Activated by enterokinase in duodenum |
| **Chymotrypsin, elastase, carboxypeptidase** | Pancreas | Proteins | Cooperate with trypsin |
| **Pancreatic lipase + colipase** | Pancreas | TG → MG + FFA | Main fat digestion; needs bile for emulsification |
| **Cholesterol esterase** | Pancreas | Cholesterol esters | Releases cholesterol |
| **Phospholipase A2** | Pancreas | Phospholipids | Releases fatty acids |
| **Lactase, sucrase, maltase, dextrinase** | Brush border | Disaccharides → monosaccharides | Final step of carb digestion |
| **Aminopeptidases, dipeptidases** | Brush border | Peptides → amino acids | Final protein digestion |
| **Enterokinase (enteropeptidase)** | Duodenal brush border | Trypsinogen → trypsin | The master switch - activates pancreatic protease cascade |

### GI hormones

| Hormone | Source | Trigger | Action | Why crucial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gastrin** | G cells (antrum) | Peptides, distension, vagal | ↑ HCl from parietal cells; trophic to mucosa | Zollinger-Ellison (gastrinoma) → recurrent ulcers |
| **CCK** | I cells (duodenum) | Fatty acids, peptides | ↑ pancreatic enzymes, ↑ gallbladder contraction, ↓ gastric emptying | Postprandial gallbladder pain in cholelithiasis |
| **Secretin** | S cells (duodenum) | Acid in duodenum | ↑ pancreatic + biliary HCO₃⁻ | Neutralizes acid; diagnostic test for gastrinoma |
| **GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic peptide)** | K cells (duodenum) | Glucose, fats | ↑ insulin (incretin) | Why oral glucose triggers more insulin than IV glucose |
| **GLP-1** | L cells (ileum) | Carbs, fats | ↑ insulin, ↓ glucagon, ↓ gastric emptying, satiety | **Target of semaglutide, tirzepatide** - diabetes + obesity drugs |
| **Motilin** | M cells (small intestine) | Fasting state | Initiates **migrating motor complex** between meals | "Housekeeper" of GI; erythromycin agonist used for gastroparesis |
| **Somatostatin** | D cells (everywhere) | Acid, food | Inhibits everything GI | Octreotide (analog) used for variceal bleeding, NETs |
| **VIP** | Enteric neurons | - | Relaxes smooth muscle, secretes water/electrolytes | VIPoma → WDHA (watery diarrhea, hypokalemia, achlorhydria) |
| **Ghrelin** | Stomach (P/D1 cells) | Fasting | ↑ appetite | "Hunger hormone"; rises before meals |

### Absorption transporters

| Nutrient | Transporter | Mechanism | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Glucose / galactose** | SGLT1 (apical), GLUT2 (basolateral) | Na⁺-coupled | Duodenum/jejunum |
| **Fructose** | GLUT5 (apical), GLUT2 (basolateral) | Facilitated | Duodenum/jejunum |
| **Amino acids** | Multiple Na⁺-coupled transporters | Active | Jejunum |
| **Di-/tripeptides** | PepT1 (H⁺-coupled) | Active | Brush border |
| **Fats (MGs, FFAs)** | Diffuse from micelles → re-esterified → chylomicrons → lacteals | Passive uptake, lymphatic exit | Jejunum |
| **Iron (Fe²⁺)** | DMT1 (apical), ferroportin (basolateral, regulated by hepcidin) | Active | Duodenum |
| **Calcium** | TRPV6 (apical), calbindin, PMCA (basolateral) - vitamin D-dependent | Active | Duodenum |
| **B12** | IF-B12 complex → cubilin receptor → endocytosis | Receptor-mediated | Terminal ileum |
| **Bile acids** | ASBT (apical) | Na⁺-coupled | Terminal ileum (enterohepatic recirculation) |
| **Water** | Aquaporins; paracellular | Osmotic | All segments - most in small intestine |

## High-yield exam patterns

<HighYield exam="nclex">
**NCLEX GI clinical priorities:**
- **TPN**: monitor glucose q4–6h; refeeding syndrome risk (↓ K⁺, Mg²⁺, PO₄³⁻) - replete before starting; never abruptly stop (rebound hypoglycemia).
- **Lactose intolerance teaching**: avoid lactose; lactase enzyme supplements; calcium + vitamin D from other sources.
- **Celiac disease**: lifelong gluten-free diet; serologies (anti-tTG, EMA) - fall after diet compliance.
- **Cholelithiasis**: 4 F's (Female, Fat, Forty, Fertile); colicky RUQ pain after fatty meal - radiates to right shoulder.
- **Bowel sound assessment**: listen each quadrant 1 min before reporting absent.
</HighYield>

<HighYield exam="step1">
**Vitamin/mineral deficiencies - high-yield combos:**
- **B12 deficiency**: pernicious anemia, terminal ileum disease (Crohn's, resection), vegan diet → megaloblastic anemia + neuro.
- **Folate deficiency**: alcoholism, pregnancy, methotrexate, phenytoin → megaloblastic anemia (no neuro).
- **Iron deficiency**: microcytic anemia; in adults, suspect GI blood loss until proven otherwise (colonoscopy).
- **Vitamin A**: night blindness, xerophthalmia, dry skin; teratogen in pregnancy.
- **Vitamin D**: rickets (kids), osteomalacia (adults).
- **Vitamin K**: prolonged PT; warfarin antagonist; newborns get IM dose.
- **Zinc**: poor wound healing, dysgeusia.
</HighYield>

<HighYield exam="pharm">
**GI motility + secretion pharm:**
- **Loperamide**: μ-opioid agonist (doesn't cross BBB); don't use in C. diff or bloody diarrhea.
- **Bismuth subsalicylate**: anti-secretory + bismuth coating; turns stool/tongue black.
- **Ondansetron**: 5-HT3 antagonist for chemo-induced nausea; QT prolongation risk.
- **Metoclopramide**: D2 antagonist + 5-HT4 agonist; for gastroparesis; tardive dyskinesia with chronic use.
- **GLP-1 agonists** (semaglutide, liraglutide, tirzepatide [GLP-1 + GIP]): ↓ gastric emptying, ↑ satiety, ↓ glucagon, ↑ insulin; weight loss + DM benefit; pancreatitis warning.
- **Octreotide**: somatostatin analog; variceal bleeding, neuroendocrine tumors, secretory diarrhea.
</HighYield>

## Small intestine - absorptive surface in detail

<Figure name="digestive/villi-microvilli" caption="Villi and microvilli of the small intestine. Finger-like villi project from the mucosa; each enterocyte has microvilli (brush border) → ~600× surface-area amplification. Each villus carries a lacteal + capillary network for fat + nutrient absorption." />

<Figure name="digestive/small-intestine-histology" caption="Histology of the small intestine. Villi protrude into the lumen; crypts of Lieberkühn sit between villi. Layers from inside out: mucosa (epithelium + lamina propria + muscularis mucosae), submucosa, muscularis externa (inner circular + outer longitudinal), serosa." />

<Figure name="digestive/small-intestine" caption="Small intestine wall showing villi (finger-like projections), microvilli (brush border on enterocytes), and intestinal layers (mucosa, submucosa, muscularis, serosa)." />

Three structural amplifications give the small intestine ~**250 m² of surface area** (a tennis court):

1. **Plicae circulares (folds of Kerckring)** - circular submucosal folds; ~3× surface area
2. **Villi** - finger-like mucosal projections; ~10× more surface area
3. **Microvilli (brush border)** - on apical enterocytes; ~20× more

Each villus contains:
- **Enterocytes** (absorption) - replaced every 3–5 days from crypt stem cells
- **Goblet cells** (mucus)
- **Capillary network** (water-soluble nutrient pickup → portal vein → liver)
- **Lacteal** (lymphatic; chylomicron pickup → systemic circulation, bypassing portal)
- **Paneth cells** (in crypts) - antimicrobial peptides (defensins, lysozyme); host-microbe gatekeepers
- **Enteroendocrine cells** - secrete CCK, secretin, GIP, GLP-1, etc.
- **M cells** (over Peyer's patches) - antigen sampling for immune surveillance

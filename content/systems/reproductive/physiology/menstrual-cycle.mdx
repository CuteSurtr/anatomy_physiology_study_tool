A 28-day cycle (give or take) tracking two synchronized stories: the **ovarian cycle** (follicle development) and the **uterine cycle** (endometrial response).

<Figure name="reproductive/menstrual-cycle" caption="Full menstrual cycle - LH/FSH, estrogen/progesterone, follicular + endometrial events synchronized." />

<Figure name="reproductive/female-reproductive" caption="Female reproductive system - ovaries, fallopian tubes, uterus, vagina." />

## Ovarian cycle

| Phase | Days | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Follicular** | 1–13 | FSH rises → cohort of follicles develops → one dominant follicle emerges → estrogen rises sharply |
| **Ovulation** | ~14 | **LH surge** triggered by sustained high estrogen (rare positive feedback) → oocyte released |
| **Luteal** | 15–28 | Corpus luteum forms → secretes progesterone (and estrogen) → if no pregnancy, luteolysis at day 26 → hormone withdrawal |

## Uterine cycle

| Phase | Days | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Menses** | 1–5 | Functional layer of endometrium shed (progesterone withdrawal) |
| **Proliferative** | 6–14 | Estrogen-driven endometrial proliferation; spiral arteries lengthen |
| **Secretory** | 15–28 | Progesterone makes endometrium glandular and receptive (~implantation window day 20–24) |

## Hormone choreography

- **Estrogen** dominates the follicular phase, rising to its preovulatory peak.
- **Progesterone** dominates the luteal phase.
- Both **inhibit FSH/LH** via negative feedback **except** the late-follicular estrogen peak which produces brief **positive** feedback → **LH surge** → ovulation.

If pregnancy occurs: implanting embryo secretes **hCG**, which rescues the corpus luteum → continued progesterone → endometrium maintained. (The hCG → corpus luteum baton is handed to the **placenta** around 8–10 weeks.)

<Note title="Why combined oral contraceptives work">
Exogenous estrogen + progestin maintain steady negative feedback → no LH surge → no ovulation. Bonus: thicken cervical mucus, thin endometrium.
</Note>

<Clinical>
**Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)**: chronic anovulation with hyperandrogenism. Elevated LH:FSH ratio, multiple small follicles arrested at antral stage. Unopposed estrogen action on the endometrium increases endometrial-cancer risk - manage with cyclic or continuous progestin.

**Premature ovarian insufficiency**: amenorrhea + high FSH before age 40. The HPG axis is *intact* - the gonads are failing.
</Clinical>

## Every hormonal player in the cycle - function reference

| Hormone | Source | Trigger / control | Effect | Why crucial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GnRH** | Hypothalamus (arcuate, pulsatile) | Neural input + feedback | ↑ FSH + LH | Pulsatility is essential - continuous GnRH suppresses (leuprolide trick) |
| **FSH** | Anterior pituitary gonadotrophs | GnRH (+); estrogen (−); inhibin B (−) | Granulosa cell proliferation; aromatase induction; follicle recruitment | Selects dominant follicle; FSH rises in menopause when follicles deplete |
| **LH** | Anterior pituitary gonadotrophs | GnRH (+); estrogen briefly (+) for surge; otherwise (−) | Theca cell androgen synthesis; ovulation trigger (surge); corpus luteum maintenance | Surge causes ovulation ~36 h after onset |
| **Estradiol (E2)** | Granulosa cells (via aromatase from theca androgens); corpus luteum | FSH-driven | Endometrial proliferation; ↑ LH receptors; positive feedback at high levels (LH surge) | Most potent natural estrogen; receptor in many tissues |
| **Estrone (E1)** | Adipose (aromatase converts androstenedione) | Adipose mass | Main postmenopausal estrogen | Why obese postmenopausal women have ↑ endometrial cancer risk |
| **Estriol (E3)** | Placenta in pregnancy | Pregnancy | Marker of fetal-placental health | ↓ in fetal aneuploidy |
| **Progesterone** | Corpus luteum; placenta after 8–10 wk | LH | Secretory endometrium; ↑ body temp (~0.5°C); ↑ cervical mucus thickness; smooth-muscle relaxation | Maintains pregnancy; drop triggers menses |
| **Inhibin B** | Granulosa cells (follicular phase) | FSH | Selectively inhibits FSH | Lets one follicle dominate |
| **Inhibin A** | Corpus luteum (luteal phase) | LH | Inhibits FSH | Maintains luteal-phase low FSH |
| **Activin** | Granulosa cells | Local | Stimulates FSH; counterpart to inhibin | Local follicle regulation |
| **AMH (anti-Müllerian hormone)** | Granulosa cells (early follicles) | Constitutive | Inhibits recruitment of additional primordial follicles | **Marker of ovarian reserve** in fertility evaluation |
| **hCG** | Syncytiotrophoblast (placenta) | Implantation | Mimics LH → maintains corpus luteum | Pregnancy test target; β-hCG used clinically |
| **Relaxin** | Corpus luteum + placenta | Pregnancy | Softens pubic symphysis, cervix | Late pregnancy ligament laxity |
| **Prolactin** | Anterior pituitary lactotrophs | TRH (+); dopamine (−) | Lactation; suppresses GnRH | Hyperprolactinemia → amenorrhea, galactorrhea, infertility |
| **Oxytocin** | Posterior pituitary | Cervical stretch; nipple suckling | Uterine contraction; milk let-down | Positive-feedback loop in parturition |

## High-yield exam patterns

<HighYield exam="nclex">
**NCLEX reproductive teaching:**
- **OCP missed dose**: 1 missed → take ASAP + next on time; 2+ missed → take 1 + backup contraception for 7 days; weeks 1 or 4 missed → consider emergency contraception.
- **Pregnancy test timing**: detectable hCG 10–11 days post-conception (urine slightly later).
- **Menopause symptoms**: hot flashes, vaginal dryness, mood, osteoporosis risk; HRT short-term for severe symptoms (weigh CV/breast cancer risk).
- **PCOS teaching**: weight loss + metformin help insulin resistance; combined OCPs for menstrual regulation + androgen blunting.
- **Fertility timing**: ovulation ~14 days **before** next menses; basal body temperature rises 0.5°C after ovulation.
</HighYield>

<HighYield exam="step1">
**Cycle disease patterns:**
- **PCOS** (Rotterdam criteria, 2 of 3): oligo/anovulation, hyperandrogenism, polycystic ovaries on US; **LH:FSH ratio &gt;2**; insulin resistance.
- **Primary amenorrhea workup**: imaging + hormones; consider Turner syndrome (45,X), androgen insensitivity, Müllerian agenesis.
- **Secondary amenorrhea**: rule out pregnancy first; then hypothalamic (exercise, stress, anorexia), PCOS, prolactinoma, premature ovarian insufficiency.
- **Premature ovarian insufficiency**: high FSH before 40; vs menopause = same labs but later.
- **Mittelschmerz**: mid-cycle pelvic pain from ovulation; benign.
</HighYield>

<HighYield exam="pharm">
**Reproductive endocrine pharm:**
- **Clomiphene**: SERM; blocks hypothalamic estrogen receptor → ↑ GnRH → ↑ FSH/LH; ovulation induction.
- **Letrozole**: aromatase inhibitor; ovulation induction (preferred in PCOS), breast cancer.
- **Tamoxifen**: SERM; breast antagonist (cancer), endometrial agonist (↑ endometrial Ca risk).
- **Anastrozole, exemestane**: aromatase inhibitors; postmenopausal breast cancer; ↓ bone density.
- **Leuprolide**: GnRH agonist; continuous dosing **suppresses** (treats fibroids, endometriosis, prostate Ca); pulsatile **stimulates**.
- **Danazol**: weak androgen; endometriosis; virilizing side effects.
- **Bromocriptine, cabergoline**: D2 agonists; prolactinoma + suppress lactation.
</HighYield>

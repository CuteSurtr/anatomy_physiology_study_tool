The reproductive system has gonads (**testes** producing sperm + testosterone; **ovaries** producing oocytes + estrogen/progesterone), ducts that transport gametes, accessory glands, and external genitalia. Function is governed by the **hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis**: GnRH → LH/FSH → gonadal sex hormones, with negative-feedback loops that differ between cyclic (ovarian) and tonic (testicular) regulation.

The two sexes are built from the **same embryonic parts**, which is why so much of the anatomy pairs up: ovary/testis, labia majora/scrotum, clitoris/penis, Skene glands/prostate. The functional difference is timing. The testis runs a **tonic** system that makes gametes continuously from puberty onward. The ovary runs a **cyclic** one: a fixed store of oocytes laid down before birth, released roughly one per month, with the whole hormonal cycle rebuilt each time.

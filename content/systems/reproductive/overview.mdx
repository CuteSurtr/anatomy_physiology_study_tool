The reproductive system has gonads (**testes** producing sperm + testosterone; **ovaries** producing oocytes + estrogen/progesterone), ducts that transport gametes, accessory glands, and external genitalia. Function is governed by the **hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis**: GnRH → LH/FSH → gonadal sex hormones, with negative-feedback loops that differ between cyclic (ovarian) and tonic (testicular) regulation.

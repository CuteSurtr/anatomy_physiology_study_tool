The adrenal glands sit on top of the kidneys like tricornered hats and contain **two completely separate organs in one capsule**: an outer **cortex** (steroid factory, mesodermal origin) and an inner **medulla** (sympathetic ganglion, neural crest origin). Each layer makes a different hormone, regulated by a different axis, with a different disease pattern.

## Gross anatomy

<Figure name="urinary/kidney-internal" caption="Related figure for context — Gross anatomy." />

- **Location**: superior pole of each kidney; **retroperitoneal**; T11–T12 level.
- **Right adrenal**: pyramid-shaped, sits posterior to IVC. Right adrenal vein drains directly into IVC (short).
- **Left adrenal**: crescent-shaped, lies medial to spleen + posterior to pancreas. Left adrenal vein drains into **left renal vein** (longer course).
- **Weight**: 4–6 g each.

### Blood supply - generous (steroid synthesis needs cholesterol)
| Artery | Source |
|---|---|
| **Superior adrenal artery** | Inferior phrenic |
| **Middle adrenal artery** | Abdominal aorta directly |
| **Inferior adrenal artery** | Renal artery |

### Innervation
- **Cortex**: minimal direct innervation; regulated hormonally (ACTH, angiotensin II, K⁺).
- **Medulla**: directly innervated by **preganglionic sympathetic fibers** (T5–T11) via the greater splanchnic nerves. Medullary chromaffin cells = modified postganglionic neurons that secrete into blood instead of onto a target → "endocrine ganglion."

## Cortex - three concentric zones, three hormone classes

<Figure name="digestive/gi-hormones-summary" caption="Related figure for context — Cortex - three concentric zones, three hormone classes." />

<Mnemonic>
**"GFR - Salt, Sugar, Sex"** (Glomerulosa → mineralocorticoids → salt; Fasciculata → glucocorticoids → sugar; Reticularis → androgens → sex). And the deeper you go, the more lipophilic.
</Mnemonic>

| Zone | % cortex | Main hormone | Regulation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zona glomerulosa** (outer) | 15% | **Aldosterone** (mineralocorticoid) | RAAS (angiotensin II + ↑ K⁺); ACTH plays minor permissive role |
| **Zona fasciculata** (middle) | 75% | **Cortisol** (glucocorticoid) | ACTH (HPA axis) - diurnal: peak at 8 AM, trough at midnight |
| **Zona reticularis** (inner) | 10% | **DHEA, androstenedione** (weak androgens) | ACTH |

All cortical hormones are derived from **cholesterol** via the steroidogenic enzyme cascade. The rate-limiting step is **StAR-mediated transport of cholesterol into mitochondria** + **desmolase (P450scc)** = side-chain cleavage to pregnenolone.

### Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (CAH) - three enzyme defects (must know)

| Enzyme defect | Mineralocorticoid | Cortisol | Androgens | BP | Phenotype |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **21-hydroxylase** (most common, 90%) | ↓ | ↓ | ↑↑ | ↓ (salt wasting) | Ambiguous female genitalia; precocious puberty in males |
| **11β-hydroxylase** | ↓ aldo, but ↑ 11-deoxycorticosterone (a weak mineralocorticoid) | ↓ | ↑↑ | **↑** (HTN) | Same virilization but hypertensive |
| **17α-hydroxylase** | ↑ (11-deoxycorticosterone) | ↓ | ↓ | ↑ (HTN) | Phenotypic female regardless of XY; no adrenarche |

Pattern: any block sends **substrate above** the block **back up** the cascade → shunted into whatever pathways remain.

## Medulla - modified sympathetic ganglion

<Figure name="endocrine/pheochromocytoma-clinical" caption="Adrenal medulla - chromaffin cells = modified postganglionic sympathetic neurons. Secrete epinephrine (~80%) + norepinephrine (~20%) into blood. Pheochromocytoma = tumor of these cells." />

| Cell | Hormone | % |
|---|---|---|
| **Chromaffin cells** | **Epinephrine (80%)** + **norepinephrine (20%)** + small amounts of dopamine | - |
| **Sustentacular cells** | Support | Few |

- Chromaffin cells synthesize catecholamines from tyrosine: tyrosine → DOPA (TH, rate-limiting) → dopamine → NE → **Epi** (PNMT, requires cortisol from neighboring zona fasciculata).
- **PNMT** (phenylethanolamine N-methyltransferase) needs cortisol - why the medulla is wrapped around the cortex and bathed in cortisol-rich blood from the **portal vascular system** of the adrenal.
- Stored in chromaffin granules; released by acetylcholine from preganglionic sympathetic fibers.

### Epinephrine vs norepinephrine receptor binding
| Receptor | Epi | NE |
|---|---|---|
| **α1** (vasoconstriction) | + | +++ |
| **α2** (presynaptic feedback) | + | ++ |
| **β1** (heart) | +++ | +++ |
| **β2** (vasodilation in muscle, bronchodilation) | +++ | – |

The β2 binding is what makes epi **so different from NE in clinical use** - epi is the drug for anaphylaxis (bronchodilation + cardiac support) while NE is the pressor for septic shock (pure α1/β1 → vasoconstriction).

## Cortisol - actions everywhere

<Figure name="endocrine/steroidogenesis-detail" caption="Cortisol synthesis (steroidogenesis) - cholesterol → pregnenolone (P450scc, ACTH-stimulated) → progesterone → 17-OH-progesterone → 11-deoxycortisol → cortisol via 11β-hydroxylase." />

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Metabolic** | ↑ gluconeogenesis, ↑ lipolysis, ↑ proteolysis → ↑ blood glucose ("**diabetogenic**"); chronic = central obesity + truncal redistribution |
| **Anti-inflammatory** | Inhibits PLA2 → ↓ arachidonic acid → ↓ prostaglandins + leukotrienes. Stabilizes lysosomal membranes. **Inhibits NF-κB**. ↓ COX-2 expression |
| **Immune** | ↓ IL-2 + Th1 response; **demargination** of neutrophils → neutrophilia on CBC; lymphocytopenia + eosinopenia |
| **Cardiovascular** | Upregulates α1 receptors → potentiates catecholamines (permissive). Addison's → vasodilation + hypotension |
| **Bone** | ↑ osteoclast activity + ↓ osteoblast → **osteoporosis** with chronic excess |
| **Skin** | ↓ collagen → thin skin + striae (Cushing) |
| **CNS** | Mood (depression, psychosis at high dose), memory |
| **GI** | ↑ acid secretion → peptic ulcer risk |
| **Maintenance of BP** | Permissive role for catecholamines + small mineralocorticoid effect |

Endogenous cortisol secretion is **pulsatile + diurnal**: peak ~8 AM, nadir ~midnight. Stress (illness, surgery, hypoglycemia) breaks the pattern.

## Aldosterone - sodium guardian

<Figure name="urinary/raas-aldosterone-openstax" caption="Aldosterone axis - zona glomerulosa cells respond to ATII + hyperkalemia → secrete aldosterone → collecting duct → ↑ ENaC + ROMK + Na/K-ATPase → Na+ retention + K+/H+ excretion." />

| Target | Action |
|---|---|
| **Principal cells (collecting duct)** | ↑ ENaC + ↑ Na/K-ATPase → Na⁺ retention + K⁺ excretion |
| **α-intercalated cells (collecting duct)** | ↑ H⁺ excretion → alkalosis trend |
| **Sweat glands, salivary glands, colon** | Similar Na⁺ retention |

Net: ↑ Na⁺ + water retention + ↑ blood volume + ↑ K⁺ loss + ↑ H⁺ loss. Triggered by AII (RAAS), ↑ K⁺, ACTH (permissive).

<Clinical>
**Cushing syndrome** (hypercortisolism): central obesity, moon face, buffalo hump, purple striae, easy bruising, HTN, hyperglycemia, osteoporosis, psychiatric. Causes: **exogenous steroids** (most common) > **pituitary adenoma (Cushing disease)** > ectopic ACTH (small cell lung cancer) > adrenal adenoma.

**Conn syndrome** (primary hyperaldosteronism): aldo-secreting adenoma → HTN + **hypokalemia** + metabolic alkalosis + suppressed renin. Screen with aldosterone/renin ratio.

**Pheochromocytoma**: catecholamine-secreting medullary tumor. Triad = **headache + sweating + palpitations** + episodic HTN. **Rule of 10s**: 10% bilateral, 10% extra-adrenal (paraganglioma), 10% malignant, 10% familial (MEN2, VHL, NF1). Treat α-block first, then β.

**Addison disease** (primary adrenal insufficiency): autoimmune adrenalitis (developed world) or TB (developing world). Loss of all 3 hormone classes. Hyperpigmentation (POMC → ACTH + MSH both elevated as cortisol negative feedback fails). Salt craving, hypotension, hypoglycemia, hyperkalemia, hyponatremia. **Diagnose**: cosyntropin (ACTH) stim test fails to raise cortisol.
</Clinical>

## Function reference - every adrenal component

| Component | Function | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Capsule** | Connective tissue boundary | Site of stem cells that regenerate the cortex |
| **Zona glomerulosa** | Aldosterone synthesis (only zone with aldosterone synthase) | Conn syndrome originates here; spironolactone blocks aldosterone effect |
| **Zona fasciculata** | Cortisol + corticosterone synthesis | Largest zone; site of most cortisol-producing adenomas; cortisol diffuses to medulla to activate PNMT |
| **Zona reticularis** | DHEA + androstenedione | Source of adrenarche (axillary + pubic hair before puberty); CAH virilization comes from here |
| **Chromaffin cells (medulla)** | Catecholamine secretion | Modified sympathetic ganglion neurons; source of pheochromocytoma |
| **StAR protein** | Transports cholesterol into mitochondria | Rate-limiting step of all steroidogenesis; deficient in lipoid CAH |
| **PNMT enzyme** | Converts NE → Epi | Requires cortisol → unique to medulla |
| **CRH-ACTH-cortisol axis** | Stress response | Disrupted in: pituitary tumor (high ACTH), exogenous steroids (low ACTH), ectopic ACTH |
| **Right adrenal vein** | Drains directly into IVC (short, fat) | Surgical hazard during right adrenalectomy |
| **Left adrenal vein** | Drains into left renal vein | Easier surgical access; site of adrenal vein sampling for Conn syndrome localization |

## Why this matters

<HighYield variant="boards">
- **Cushing workup**: (1) screen with 24-h urine free cortisol or late-night salivary cortisol or low-dose DST. (2) Then ACTH: low = adrenal source; high = pituitary or ectopic. (3) High-dose DST: pituitary tumor still suppresses; ectopic doesn't.
- **CRH-ACTH-cortisol negative feedback** is why long-term oral steroids cause adrenal atrophy + suppression. Taper slowly; sick-day stress doses needed.
- **Sweat Cl⁻ in CAH** - not relevant. But aldosterone-driven Na⁺ retention via ENaC means **amiloride + spironolactone** block aldo effect → useful in Liddle, Conn.
- **MEN syndromes** - MEN2A: medullary thyroid CA + pheochromocytoma + parathyroid hyperplasia. MEN2B: same minus parathyroid, plus mucosal neuromas + marfanoid habitus. Both: **RET proto-oncogene** mutation. Always check calcitonin + metanephrines.
- **Cortisol's permissive role on catecholamines** → Addisonian crisis = shock unresponsive to pressors until you give **hydrocortisone**.
- **Neuroblastoma**: childhood tumor from adrenal medulla / sympathetic chain. N-myc amplification. ↑ urine HVA + VMA. Distinguished from Wilms (renal) by age and HVA/VMA.
- **5α-reductase deficiency**: XY infants raised female due to lack of DHT; virilize at puberty when testosterone rises. Not a CAH.
</HighYield>

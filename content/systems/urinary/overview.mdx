The urinary system **filters ~180 L of plasma per day** and returns ~99% of it, excreting the remaining ~1.5 L as urine. Beyond waste removal, the kidneys regulate water balance, electrolytes, acid–base status, blood pressure (via renin), and erythropoiesis (via EPO), and activate vitamin D (calcitriol). The functional unit is the **nephron** - each kidney has about a million.

## The lecture's anatomy sequence

The slide deck organizes the urinary system into three linked flows:

1. **Blood flow** into and out of the kidney
2. **Filtrate flow** through the nephron
3. **Urine flow** through the collecting system, ureters, bladder, and urethra

That framing is useful because every disease or drug in the urinary system affects one of those three pathways first.

The respiratory system has a **conducting zone** (nose → pharynx → larynx → trachea → bronchi → terminal bronchioles) that warms, humidifies, and filters air, and a **respiratory zone** (respiratory bronchioles → alveolar ducts → alveoli) where gas exchange happens. There are ~480 million alveoli giving ~70 m² of surface area - about the size of a tennis court.

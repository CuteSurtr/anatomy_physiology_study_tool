---

Three muscle tissue types: **skeletal** (voluntary, striated, multinucleated - moves the skeleton), **cardiac** (involuntary, striated, branched, intercalated discs - heart only), and **smooth** (involuntary, non-striated - walls of hollow organs and vessels). The skeletal-muscle naming convention encodes location (*biceps brachii*), action (*flexor digitorum*), shape (*deltoid*), or origin/insertion (*sternocleidomastoid*).

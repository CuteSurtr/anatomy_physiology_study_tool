**Innate immunity** is the body's hardwired, fast (minutes–hours), non-specific first line of defense. It uses **germline-encoded receptors** - pattern recognition receptors (PRRs) - to detect conserved microbial structures (PAMPs) or damaged self (DAMPs). No memory. No specificity to individual pathogens. But it's the entire defense for the first 4–7 days while adaptive immunity ramps up - and it directs adaptive responses through cytokines.

## Three pillars of innate immunity

1. **Physical and chemical barriers** (skin, mucous membranes, secretions)
2. **Cells** (neutrophils, macrophages, dendritic cells, NK cells, mast cells, eosinophils, basophils)
3. **Soluble factors** (complement, cytokines, acute-phase proteins)

## Physical and chemical barriers

| Barrier | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Skin** | Stratified squamous keratinized; low pH (4–6); fatty acids from sebaceous glands |
| **Mucous membranes** | Goblet cell mucus traps pathogens; lysozyme in tears + saliva (degrades peptidoglycan) |
| **Cilia of airway** | Mucociliary escalator - sweeps pathogens upward to be swallowed |
| **Stomach acid** | pH ~2 kills most ingested bacteria |
| **Normal flora** | Compete with pathogens; produce bacteriocins |
| **Lactoferrin** | Sequesters iron in milk + secretions |
| **Defensins** | Antimicrobial peptides in skin + neutrophil granules + Paneth cells of intestine |

### Phagocytes
| Cell | % WBC | Lifespan | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Neutrophils** | 50–70% | Hours–days in tissue | First responders to bacterial infection; multi-lobed nucleus; granules with myeloperoxidase, defensins, lactoferrin |
| **Macrophages** | (tissue resident) | Months–years | Phagocytosis + antigen presentation + cytokine secretion; Kupffer (liver), microglia (CNS), osteoclasts (bone), histiocytes (skin), alveolar (lung) |
| **Monocytes** | 2–8% | Days in blood, then differentiate to tissue macrophages | Bridge blood → tissue |
| **Dendritic cells** | &lt;1% | Days–weeks | **Most potent antigen-presenting cells**; migrate to lymph nodes to prime T cells; bridge innate → adaptive |

### Granulocytes / non-phagocytic
| Cell | Function |
|---|---|
| **NK cells** | Kill virus-infected + tumor cells **without prior sensitization**; "missing self" - kill cells lacking MHC class I |
| **Mast cells** | Tissue cells with histamine granules; trigger anaphylaxis + allergic inflammation |
| **Basophils** | Circulating equivalent of mast cells |
| **Eosinophils** | Anti-parasite + allergy; major basic protein (MBP) |
| **ILCs** (innate lymphoid cells) | Recently described; resemble T helper subsets but lack TCR; mucosal immunity |

## Pattern recognition - how innate immunity "sees"

| PRR | Location | Recognizes |
|---|---|---|
| **TLR2** | Cell surface | Lipopeptides + lipoteichoic acid (Gram+) |
| **TLR3** | Endosome | dsRNA (viruses) |
| **TLR4** | Cell surface | **LPS** (Gram-) - sepsis pathway |
| **TLR5** | Cell surface | Flagellin |
| **TLR7/8** | Endosome | ssRNA (viruses) |
| **TLR9** | Endosome | Unmethylated CpG DNA (bacteria + viruses) |
| **NLR (NLRP3)** | Cytoplasm | Cell damage signals → **inflammasome** assembly → caspase-1 → IL-1β + IL-18 |
| **RIG-I / MDA5** | Cytoplasm | Viral RNA |
| **cGAS-STING** | Cytoplasm | Cytosolic dsDNA → type I interferon |
| **Mannose receptor** (macrophages) | Surface | Mannose on microbial glycans |
| **Scavenger receptors** | Surface | Modified LDL, apoptotic cells |

PAMPs (pathogen-associated molecular patterns) trigger PRRs. DAMPs (damage-associated) come from injured cells (ATP, HMGB1, uric acid crystals) - explain sterile inflammation (e.g., gout, ischemic injury).

## Complement system - three pathways converge

<Figure name="lymphatic/complement" caption="Complement cascade - classical (antibody-Ag), lectin (MBL), and alternative pathways converge at C3 convertase → MAC formation (C5b-9), opsonization, and anaphylatoxins (C3a, C5a)." />


Complement is a cascade of ~30 plasma proteins synthesized by the liver, normally inactive until cleaved.

| Pathway | Activator |
|---|---|
| **Classical** | Antibody-antigen complexes (**IgM, IgG1, IgG3**) - bridge to adaptive immunity |
| **Lectin** | Mannose-binding lectin (MBL) binds microbial mannose |
| **Alternative** | Spontaneous C3 hydrolysis; stabilized on microbial surfaces (lack of complement regulators) |

All three converge at **C3 → C3a + C3b**, then **C5 → C5a + C5b → C6 + C7 + C8 + C9** = **membrane attack complex (MAC)** that lyses Gram-negative bacteria.

### Complement functions - "MAC OPI"
- **M**embrane attack complex (C5b–9) - direct lysis
- **A**naphylatoxins (C3a, C5a, C4a) - mast cell degranulation, chemotaxis
- **C**hemotaxis (especially C5a - neutrophil magnet)
- **O**psonization (C3b coats pathogens for phagocytosis)
- **P**athogen recognition (initiation)
- **I**mmune complex clearance (CR1 on RBCs binds C3b-tagged complexes → liver/spleen disposal)

### Deficiencies and their patterns
| Deficiency | Susceptibility |
|---|---|
| **Early (C1, C2, C4)** | Immune complex diseases (SLE) + pyogenic infections |
| **C3** | Severe recurrent pyogenic infections (loss of opsonization) |
| **Late (C5–C9 - MAC)** | **Neisseria meningitidis** + N. gonorrhoeae (encapsulated, MAC-dependent) |
| **C1 esterase inhibitor** | Hereditary angioedema - episodic facial/laryngeal swelling; bradykinin-mediated |
| **DAF (CD55), CD59** | Paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH) - complement-mediated RBC lysis |

## Inflammation - the orchestrated response

<Figure name="integumentary/wound-healing-phases" caption="Acute inflammation as the first protective response — vasodilation + permeability + leukocyte migration. Resolution returns tissue to baseline; persistence drives chronic inflammation." />

### Acute inflammation - five cardinal signs (Celsus + Galen)
**Rubor** (redness), **tumor** (swelling), **calor** (heat), **dolor** (pain), **functio laesa** (loss of function).

### Vascular events (minutes)
- **Transient vasoconstriction** → **vasodilation** (histamine, NO, prostaglandins) → ↑ blood flow (heat, redness)
- **↑ vascular permeability** → exudation of protein-rich fluid (edema, swelling)

### Cellular events (hours)
**Leukocyte extravasation** - 4 steps:
1. **Margination + rolling** - selectins (P-, E-selectin on endothelium; sialyl-Lewis X on leukocytes)
2. **Adhesion** - integrins (LFA-1, Mac-1 on leukocytes ↔ ICAM-1, VCAM-1 on endothelium)
3. **Diapedesis** - squeeze between endothelial cells (PECAM-1)
4. **Chemotaxis** - follow gradient (IL-8, C5a, LTB4, bacterial peptides)

| Step defect | Disease |
|---|---|
| **LAD-1** (CD18 integrin defect) | Recurrent pyogenic infections, no pus, delayed umbilical cord separation, ↑↑ WBC |
| **Chediak-Higashi** (LYST gene → lysosomal trafficking) | Giant granules, partial albinism, peripheral neuropathy, recurrent infection |
| **Chronic granulomatous disease (CGD)** | NADPH oxidase defect → ↓ reactive oxygen species → no oxidative burst; recurrent catalase+ organisms (S. aureus, Aspergillus, Burkholderia, Serratia, Nocardia) |

## Cytokines - the chemical language

| Cytokine | Source | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **IL-1** | Macrophages | Acute phase, fever, T cell costim |
| **IL-6** | Macrophages, T cells | Acute phase (CRP, fibrinogen), B cell differentiation, fever |
| **IL-8 / CXCL8** | Macrophages | **Neutrophil chemotaxis** |
| **IL-12** | Macrophages, dendritic cells | Drives Th1 + NK cells |
| **TNF-α** | Macrophages | Fever, cachexia, septic shock; vascular leak; vasodilation |
| **Type I interferons (IFN-α/β)** | All cells (virus-infected) | Antiviral state - slows viral replication; activates NK cells |
| **IFN-γ** | Th1, NK | Activates macrophages; granuloma formation |
| **TGF-β** | T regs, macrophages | Anti-inflammatory; tissue repair; fibrosis |

### Hot vs cold cytokines mnemonic
**"Hot T-Bone stEAk"** - **IL-1** fever, **TNF-α** wasting; **IL-2** stimulates T cells, **IL-3** stimulates Bone marrow, **IL-4** IgE class switch, **IL-5** stimulates Eosinophils + IgA.

## Acute phase reactants (liver - IL-6 driven)

<Figure name="cardiovascular/coagulation-simple" caption="Acute phase reactants - liver-produced under IL-6. Positive APRs (CRP, fibrinogen, ferritin, hepcidin, complement) rise; negative APRs (albumin, transferrin) fall." />

| ↑ | ↓ |
|---|---|
| CRP, ESR, fibrinogen, ferritin, hepcidin, haptoglobin, serum amyloid A, complement | Albumin, transferrin |

CRP rises within 6 h, peaks 48 h. ESR is non-specific. Ferritin and hepcidin explain **anemia of chronic disease** (iron sequestered).

## Function reference - every innate component

| Component | Function | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Neutrophils** | Phagocytose bacteria; oxidative burst via NADPH oxidase | Neutropenia (&lt;500) → urgent risk of bacteremia |
| **Macrophages** | Phagocytose + present antigen + cytokine factory | Drive granuloma formation (TB, sarcoid) |
| **Dendritic cells** | "Sentinels" - bring antigen to lymph node | Bridge to adaptive immunity |
| **NK cells** | Kill virus-infected + tumor cells lacking MHC-I | Activated by IFN-α/β; granzymes + perforin |
| **Mast cells** | Histamine + tryptase + heparin granules | Anaphylaxis; chronic urticaria; mastocytosis |
| **C3b** | Major opsonin | Loss = pyogenic infections |
| **C5a** | Chemoattractant + anaphylatoxin | Major signal in neutrophil recruitment |
| **MAC (C5b–9)** | Pore in Gram- bacteria | Loss → Neisseria infections |
| **TLR4** | LPS sensor | Activates NF-κB → cytokine storm; target in sepsis |
| **NLRP3 inflammasome** | Cleaves pro-IL-1β to active form | Gout (urate crystals), familial Mediterranean fever, autoinflammatory diseases |
| **Type I IFN (α/β)** | Antiviral state in all nucleated cells | Suppressed by some viruses (vaccinia, HIV); used therapeutically (hep B, MS) |
| **Lysozyme** | Cleaves peptidoglycan | Tears, saliva, neutrophil granules |
| **MBL** | Lectin pathway initiator | Deficiency → pyogenic infections in infancy |

## Why this matters

<HighYield variant="boards">
- **Chronic granulomatous disease (CGD)** - NADPH oxidase defect → can't kill catalase-positive organisms (S. aureus, Aspergillus, Burkholderia, Serratia, Nocardia - mnemonic "**Cats Need PLACESS**"). Diagnose with **DHR test** (failed fluorescence) or **NBT test**.
- **Neisseria meningitidis** infection in a young adult with recurrent meningococcemia → screen complement C5–C9.
- **Hereditary angioedema** - C1 esterase inhibitor deficiency. Episodic non-pruritic swelling. ACE inhibitors precipitate. Treat acute attacks with **C1 INH concentrate** or **icatibant** (bradykinin B2 antagonist). NOT epinephrine/steroid-responsive.
- **Sepsis** - innate immune cascade gone awry. TLR4 activation by LPS → massive TNF-α + IL-1 + IL-6 release → vasodilation, capillary leak, DIC.
- **Asplenia** - loss of marginal zone + opsonin synthesis → encapsulated organisms = the bacteria innate immunity barely catches without antibody help.
- **Job syndrome (Hyper-IgE)**: STAT3 mutation → Th17 defect → recurrent **cold abscesses** + eczema + ↑ IgE + retained primary teeth + coarse facies. Limited inflammatory response = "cold."
- **MPO deficiency**: usually asymptomatic but ↑ susceptibility to **Candida**.
- **Anti-G6PD (gout-CGD)** - wait, that's wrong. Just remember: G6PD deficiency → ↓ NADPH → ↓ glutathione → hemolysis with oxidants. Different from CGD even though both use NADPH.
</HighYield>

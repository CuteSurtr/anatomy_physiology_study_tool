The skin is the **largest organ** (~1.5–2 m², ~16% of body weight) and the body's first interface with the environment. Five jobs: **barrier** (mechanical + microbial), **thermoregulation** (sweat, dermal vasoconstriction/dilation), **sensation** (Meissner, Pacinian, Merkel, Ruffini), **vitamin D synthesis** (UV-driven), and **excretion** (minor). Three layers from superficial to deep: **epidermis** (stratified squamous keratinized), **dermis** (papillary + reticular), **hypodermis** (subcutaneous fat).

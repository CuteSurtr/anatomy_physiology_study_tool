The skin is not just a passive barrier - it contains **epidermal appendages** that grow from invaginations of the embryologic epidermis into the dermis: **hair follicles, nails, sebaceous glands, sweat glands**. These structures handle thermoregulation, secretion, sensation, and aesthetics - and have their own clinical pathology + pharmacology.

## Hair follicle

### Anatomy
- **Hair shaft** - keratinized; medulla (center) + cortex + cuticle.
- **Hair root** - within the follicle; ends in the **hair bulb** containing the **matrix** (proliferating keratinocytes + melanocytes).
- **Dermal papilla** - vascular projection at base of bulb; provides nutrients + inductive signals.
- **Arrector pili muscle** - smooth muscle; sympathetic adrenergic stimulation → contraction → goosebumps ("piloerection"); thermoregulation in furred mammals + emotional response (fear).
- **Sebaceous gland** opens onto the upper follicle.
- **Bulge region** - stem cell niche at the level of arrector pili attachment; supplies follicle + adjacent epidermis.

### Hair cycle (asynchronous across body)
| Phase | Duration | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Anagen** (growth) | 2–6 y for scalp | Matrix proliferating; ~85% of scalp hairs |
| **Catagen** (regression) | 2–3 weeks | Follicle shrinks; ~1% |
| **Telogen** (rest) | 2–4 months | Club hair; ~14% |
| **Exogen** | Days–weeks | Shedding |

Eyebrows + body hair have much shorter anagen → shorter terminal length.

### Hair types
- **Lanugo** - fine, unpigmented; fetal; sheds before birth (or shortly after). Re-appears in **anorexia nervosa** + paraneoplastic syndromes.
- **Vellus** - short, fine, pale ("peach fuzz"); covers most of body in children + women.
- **Terminal** - long, thick, pigmented; scalp, eyebrows, axilla, pubic, beard, chest.

Androgens (testosterone → DHT via 5α-reductase) convert vellus to terminal at puberty in androgen-sensitive areas (beard, chest); in androgen-sensitive scalp areas, the opposite occurs in **androgenetic alopecia** (terminal → vellus → miniaturization).

### Common hair disorders
| Disease | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Androgenetic alopecia** (male-pattern, female-pattern) | DHT-driven follicular miniaturization in genetically predisposed scalp areas. Tx: minoxidil (topical), finasteride (5α-reductase inhibitor) |
| **Alopecia areata** | Autoimmune attack on anagen follicles → "exclamation point hairs"; smooth patches |
| **Telogen effluvium** | Stress (illness, postpartum, surgery) shifts anagen hairs into telogen → shedding 2–3 months later |
| **Hirsutism** | Terminal hair in androgen-dependent areas in women (PCOS, CAH, ovarian/adrenal tumors) |
| **Tinea capitis** | Dermatophyte (Trichophyton, Microsporum) → broken hairs + scale + alopecia patches |

## Nails

<Figure name="integumentary/nail-anatomy" caption="Fingernail anatomy (Blausen) - nail plate (the visible nail, dead keratinized cells), nail bed (vascular tissue beneath, gives the pink color), lunula (white crescent at base = visible part of matrix), cuticle (eponychium) protecting the matrix, free edge distally, hyponychium underneath." />

| Structure | Description |
|---|---|
| **Nail plate** | The visible "nail" - hard keratin (different from soft hair keratin) |
| **Nail bed** | Epithelium beneath plate; vascular → pink color |
| **Nail matrix** | Generates nail plate; located under proximal nail fold + **lunula** (the visible white crescent) |
| **Cuticle (eponychium)** | Skin seal at the proximal nail fold |
| **Hyponychium** | Skin under the free edge |
| **Lateral folds** | Skin grooves on each side |

Fingernails grow ~3 mm/month; toenails ~1 mm/month. Whole fingernail replaces in ~6 months.

### Nail clinical findings
| Finding | Cause |
|---|---|
| **Clubbing** | Chronic hypoxia (cyanotic heart disease, COPD, IPF, IBD, cirrhosis, lung CA) |
| **Spoon nails (koilonychia)** | Iron deficiency |
| **Pitting** | Psoriasis, alopecia areata |
| **Onycholysis** | Distal separation; psoriasis, fungal, hyperthyroid |
| **Splinter hemorrhages** | Endocarditis, trauma |
| **Beau lines** | Transverse depressions; severe systemic illness (paused matrix growth) |
| **Mees lines** | Transverse white bands; arsenic, thallium, severe illness |
| **Onychomycosis** | Dermatophyte nail infection |
| **Subungual melanoma** | Hutchinson sign - pigment extends to proximal nail fold |

## Sebaceous glands

- **Holocrine** secretion (entire cell becomes the product → ruptures + dies).
- Open into hair follicle (pilosebaceous unit) - so **scalp, face, chest, upper back** densely populated.
- Found everywhere except palms + soles + dorsum of feet.
- Secrete **sebum**: triglycerides + wax esters + squalene + cholesterol. Functions: lubricate skin + hair; mild antibacterial.
- Stimulated by **androgens** (puberty surge); fetal sebum = **vernix caseosa** covering newborn.

### Acne vulgaris - 4 mechanisms
1. **Follicular hyperkeratinization** → comedone formation (whitehead/blackhead)
2. **Excess sebum** (androgen-driven)
3. **Cutibacterium acnes** (formerly *Propionibacterium acnes*) proliferation in the sebaceous lipid medium
4. **Inflammation** (TLR2-driven)

### Acne treatments by target
| Drug | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Benzoyl peroxide** | Bactericidal + comedolytic |
| **Topical retinoids** (tretinoin, adapalene) | Normalize keratinization; ↓ comedones |
| **Salicylic acid** | Comedolytic, mild keratolytic |
| **Topical antibiotics** (clindamycin) | C. acnes |
| **Oral antibiotics** (doxycycline) | C. acnes + anti-inflammatory |
| **Combined oral contraceptives** | ↓ androgen effect |
| **Spironolactone** | Androgen antagonist (female adult acne) |
| **Isotretinoin** | All four mechanisms - ↓ sebaceous gland size + cycle; **teratogenic** (iPLEDGE program) |

## Sweat glands

### Two types
| Type | Where | Secretion | Innervation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eccrine** (merocrine) | Everywhere; densest on palms, soles, forehead, axilla | **Watery, hypotonic** (NaCl-rich initial, reabsorbed in duct) - thermoregulation | **Sympathetic cholinergic** (paradoxical - sympathetic but ACh) |
| **Apocrine** | Axilla, groin, areola, perianal | Lipid-rich, viscous; bacteria degrade → body odor | Sympathetic adrenergic |

### Eccrine sweat composition
- Initial sweat from secretory coil: isotonic NaCl + K + lactate + urea.
- Duct reabsorbs Na + Cl via ENaC + CFTR → final sweat is hypotonic.
- **Cystic fibrosis**: CFTR defective → can't reabsorb Cl⁻ → high-salt sweat → **sweat chloride test (>60 mEq/L)** diagnostic.

### Sympathetic cholinergic paradox
Eccrine glands receive sympathetic fibers but use **ACh** as neurotransmitter (not NE). This is why **anticholinergics** (atropine, antimuscarinics, TCAs) cause dry skin (anhidrosis). Spinal cord injury patients have anhidrosis below the lesion → impaired thermoregulation.

### Apocrine glands
- Develop at puberty.
- Open into hair follicles in axilla, groin.
- Secretion is sterile + odorless until bacteria decompose it → body odor.
- **Hidradenitis suppurativa**: chronic occlusion + inflammation of apocrine follicles in axillae + groin → painful nodules, sinuses, scarring. Tx: weight loss, antibiotics, biologics (adalimumab), surgery.

### Other modified glands
| Gland | Type | Location | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mammary** | Modified apocrine | Breast | Milk production |
| **Ceruminous** | Modified apocrine | External auditory canal | Cerumen (earwax) |
| **Ciliary glands of Moll** | Modified apocrine | Eyelid margins | Secretion onto eyelashes; inflammation = stye (with sebaceous Zeis gland) |
| **Meibomian (tarsal)** | Sebaceous | Eyelid | Tear film lipid; chalazion when blocked |

## Function reference - every appendage

| Structure | Function | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Hair shaft** | Insulation, sensation, protection | Loss = vulnerability + cosmetic concern |
| **Hair bulge stem cells** | Source of follicle + epidermal repair | Target in alopecia areata; lost in cicatricial alopecia |
| **Arrector pili** | Piloerection - minor thermoregulation; emotional response | Sympathetic α1; tightens follicle |
| **Sebaceous gland** | Sebum production - barrier + microbe-balancing | Acne pathogenesis; isotretinoin target |
| **Eccrine sweat gland** | Thermoregulatory; up to 1 L/h in heavy exercise | CFTR mutation → ↑ Cl⁻ in sweat (CF diagnostic) |
| **Apocrine gland** | Pheromonal + thermal | Hidradenitis suppurativa origin |
| **Nail matrix** | Continuous nail keratin production | Beau lines = matrix arrest |
| **Nail bed** | Vascular bed → nail color | Splinter hemorrhages in endocarditis |
| **Hyponychium** | Distal seal protecting subungual space | Site of subungual hematoma after trauma |
| **Lunula** | Visible distal portion of matrix | Reduced or absent in some systemic illnesses |
| **Free nerve endings around follicle** | Touch + pain | Many sensations originate from follicle deflection |
| **Meissner corpuscles** (dermal papillae) | Light touch + low-frequency vibration | Most numerous in glabrous skin (fingertips) |
| **Pacinian corpuscles** (deep dermis/hypodermis) | Deep pressure + high-frequency vibration | Concentric onion-skin layers |
| **Ruffini corpuscles** | Skin stretch + sustained pressure | - |
| **Merkel discs** | Sustained light touch + texture | Discriminative touch; Merkel cell carcinoma - neuroendocrine, aggressive |

## Why this matters

<HighYield variant="boards">
- **Cystic fibrosis** - sweat chloride >60 mEq/L is diagnostic. Pediatric salty taste when kissed.
- **Hypertrichosis** - generalized hair growth (drugs: minoxidil, cyclosporine, phenytoin; congenital); paraneoplastic lanugo with internal CA.
- **Hirsutism** - male-pattern terminal hair in females. PCOS (most common), CAH, Cushing, androgen-secreting tumors. Free testosterone + DHEA-S panel.
- **Pseudofolliculitis barbae** - curly hair re-enters skin after shaving → inflammation; common in Black men.
- **Folliculitis** (pustules around follicle) - usually S. aureus or Pseudomonas (hot tub).
- **Carbuncle** - multiple confluent furuncles; S. aureus.
- **Hidradenitis suppurativa** - recurrent painful axillary/groin nodules; chronic and undertreated.
- **Isotretinoin** - teratogen (iPLEDGE registry mandatory). Pre-treatment lipids + LFTs; pregnancy test x 2 before starting in females.
- **Acanthosis nigricans** - velvety hyperpigmentation in body folds → insulin resistance or visceral malignancy (especially gastric).
- **Erythema gyratum repens** - paraneoplastic wavy concentric rings → think lung CA.
- **Sweat-related disorders**: hyperhidrosis (treat with topical aluminum chloride, glycopyrrolate, botulinum), anhidrosis (diabetic + spinal injury risk for hyperthermia).
- **Pediculosis capitis** (head lice): contact transmission; permethrin shampoo + nit combing.
- **Scabies** (Sarcoptes scabiei): pruritic burrows in finger webs, wrists, axillae, genitals; permethrin or oral ivermectin.
</HighYield>

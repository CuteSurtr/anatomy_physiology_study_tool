Embryology explains why anatomy is the way it is - why the heart pumps blood the way it does, why your gut has the blood supply it has, and why so many congenital syndromes cluster in characteristic ways. The first **8 weeks** (the embryonic period) is when organs form; weeks 9–38 = fetal period (growth + maturation). Most teratogenic insults strike weeks 3–8.

<Figure name="foundations/embryo-cleavage" caption="First two weeks of development - zygote → morula → blastocyst → implantation." />

<Figure name="foundations/germ-layers" caption="Gastrulation establishes ectoderm, mesoderm, and endoderm, which then differentiate into the major organ systems." />

Read the first two figures as a sequence: **cleavage tells you where the embryo comes from; gastrulation tells you what every tissue can become**. Most congenital-malformation questions are really asking which embryonic population failed to migrate, fuse, close, or remodel.

## Week-by-week milestones (memorize the highlights)

| Week | Event |
|---|---|
| **1** | Fertilization → zygote → cleavage → morula → blastocyst → **implantation** (day 6–7) |
| **2** | Bilaminar disc (**epiblast + hypoblast**); "**2 weeks = 2 layers, 2 cavities (amniotic + yolk sac), 2 components (cytotrophoblast + syncytiotrophoblast)**" |
| **3** | **Gastrulation** → trilaminar disc (ectoderm, mesoderm, endoderm); **primitive streak**; notochord forms; neural plate; cardiac field activates |
| **4** | **Neural tube closure** (cranial day 25, caudal day 28); somites; **heart begins beating** (day 22); 4 limb buds |
| **5–6** | Limb buds elongate; embryo folds (cylinder shape); upper limb starts before lower |
| **7** | Digits differentiate; eyelids form |
| **8** | All major organs present; embryo recognizable as human → "fetus" |
| **10** | External genitalia differentiate by sex |
| **22–24** | Lungs develop alveolar precursors → viability threshold |

## The germ layers - what becomes what

### Ectoderm (outer)
| Subtype | Derivatives |
|---|---|
| **Surface ectoderm** | Epidermis + skin appendages (hair, nails, sweat glands); anterior pituitary (Rathke's pouch); inner ear sensory cells; lens of eye; **enamel of teeth**; epithelium of mouth + anal canal below pectinate |
| **Neuroectoderm (neural tube)** | CNS (brain + spinal cord); retina + optic nerve; posterior pituitary; pineal gland |
| **Neural crest** | PNS (sensory ganglia, autonomic ganglia, Schwann cells); melanocytes; **chromaffin cells of adrenal medulla**; craniofacial bones + cartilage; aorticopulmonary septum (truncus + bulbus); odontoblasts; parafollicular C cells; enteric ganglia |

### Mesoderm (middle)
| Subtype | Derivatives |
|---|---|
| **Paraxial (somites)** | Bone, skeletal muscle (myotome), dermis of back (dermatome), vertebrae, sclerotome |
| **Intermediate** | Kidneys, ureters, gonads + ducts |
| **Lateral plate - somatic** | Body wall, parietal pleura/peritoneum/pericardium, limb bones + connective tissue |
| **Lateral plate - splanchnic** | Smooth muscle + serosa of gut, cardiovascular system (heart, blood vessels), spleen (mesenchyme), visceral pleura/peritoneum/pericardium |

### Endoderm (inner)
- Gut tube epithelium (esophagus → upper anal canal)
- Lungs (epithelium only - surrounding mesoderm becomes vessels + smooth muscle)
- Liver + gallbladder + pancreas (epithelium)
- Thyroid + parathyroid + thymus follicular cells (3rd + 4th pouches)
- Bladder + urethra (urogenital sinus)
- Middle ear cavity + Eustachian tube
- Tympanic membrane (with all three germ layers - endoderm inside, mesoderm middle, ectoderm outside)

<Mnemonic>
**"Calcitonin Calls the Crest"** - parafollicular C cells = neural crest (not endoderm like the follicular cells they live with). Important boards trap.
</Mnemonic>

## Critical periods of teratogen susceptibility

| Time | Effect |
|---|---|
| **0–2 weeks** | "All or nothing" - death OR complete recovery |
| **3–8 weeks** | **Maximum susceptibility to major structural malformation** |
| **9–38 weeks** | Functional defects + minor anomalies (growth, CNS maturation) |

### Top teratogens by exposure
| Agent | Defect |
|---|---|
| **Alcohol** | Fetal alcohol syndrome - microcephaly, smooth philtrum, thin upper lip, narrow palpebral fissures, growth retardation, intellectual disability |
| **Phenytoin** | Fetal hydantoin syndrome - cleft palate, microcephaly, distal hypoplasia of digits |
| **Valproate, carbamazepine** | Neural tube defects (spina bifida); folate antagonism |
| **Warfarin** | Fetal warfarin syndrome - nasal hypoplasia, stippled epiphyses, CNS bleeds |
| **Isotretinoin (vitamin A excess)** | Craniofacial, cardiac, CNS, thymic defects |
| **ACE inhibitors / ARBs** | Renal damage, oligohydramnios, limb defects (2nd + 3rd trimester) |
| **Tetracyclines** | Yellow teeth + bone growth inhibition |
| **Aminoglycosides** | CN VIII damage |
| **Thalidomide** | Limb defects (phocomelia) |
| **DES (diethylstilbestrol)** | Clear cell vaginal adenocarcinoma in daughters |
| **Lithium** | Ebstein anomaly (atrialized right ventricle) |
| **Methotrexate** | Multiple anomalies, miscarriage |
| **Maternal diabetes** | Caudal regression, transposition of great vessels, NTD, macrosomia |
| **Maternal PKU** | Microcephaly + intellectual disability + cardiac |
| **CMV** | Hearing loss, intracerebral calcifications, microcephaly |
| **Rubella** | "Blueberry muffin" rash, PDA, cataracts, deafness |
| **Toxoplasmosis** | Hydrocephalus, chorioretinitis, calcifications |
| **Syphilis** | Saddle nose, mulberry molars, Hutchinson teeth, snuffles |
| **Zika** | Microcephaly |
| **Folate deficiency** | Neural tube defects |

## Major systems - capsule embryology

### Heart (week 3–8)
1. **Cardiogenic field** mesoderm → primitive heart tube (day 21) → begins beating (day 22).
2. **Looping** (rightward) → folds tube into atrial-ventricular alignment.
3. **Septation**:
   - Atria: **septum primum** grows down, ostium primum closes, ostium secundum opens; **septum secundum** grows on right; foramen ovale = the gap.
   - Ventricles: **muscular septum** + **membranous septum** (from endocardial cushions) → most common defect site for VSD.
   - **Truncus arteriosus**: spiraling **aorticopulmonary septum** (from neural crest) splits truncus → aorta + pulmonary trunk.

### Fetal shunts → adult remnants
| Fetal | Function | Adult remnant |
|---|---|---|
| **Umbilical vein** | Oxygenated blood placenta → fetus | Ligamentum teres hepatis |
| **Ductus venosus** | Bypass liver | Ligamentum venosum |
| **Foramen ovale** | RA → LA (bypass lungs) | Fossa ovalis |
| **Ductus arteriosus** | PA → aorta (bypass lungs) | Ligamentum arteriosum |
| **Umbilical arteries** | Deoxygenated blood fetus → placenta | Medial umbilical ligaments (NOT median, which is urachus → from allantois) |
| **Allantois → urachus** | Embryonic urinary | Median umbilical ligament |

PDA stays open in: prematurity, congenital rubella, high altitude. Closed by **prostaglandin inhibitors** (indomethacin); kept open in ductal-dependent cardiac lesions with **PGE1** (alprostadil).

### Pharyngeal apparatus (week 4)

<Figure name="foundations/pharyngeal-arches" caption="Pharyngeal arches developing in sequence, with their associated arch arteries and cranial nerves. The key memory task is matching each arch to its nerve and major derivative." />

| Arch | Cartilage | Muscles | Nerve | Artery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** (Meckel + Reichert) | Meckel cartilage → mandible; **malleus + incus** | Muscles of mastication, mylohyoid, ant belly digastric, tensor tympani | **V** (trigeminal - mandibular + maxillary divisions) | Maxillary |
| **2** | Stapes; styloid process; lesser horn of hyoid | Muscles of facial expression, stapedius, post belly digastric, stylohyoid | **VII** (facial) | Stapedial + hyoid (mostly disappears) |
| **3** | Greater horn of hyoid | Stylopharyngeus | **IX** (glossopharyngeal) | Common carotid + internal carotid |
| **4** | Laryngeal cartilages (thyroid) | Pharyngeal constrictors + cricothyroid + levator palatini | **X - superior laryngeal** | Right subclavian; left aortic arch |
| **6** | Cricoid + arytenoid + corniculate | Intrinsic laryngeal (except cricothyroid) | **X - recurrent laryngeal** | Right pulmonary; left ductus arteriosus |

<Mnemonic>
**Arch CN order**: V → VII → IX → X-sup → X-rec. "**A 1**ce **2**rry **3** **4** vagus **6**." Or: arches 1, 2, 3 = V, VII, IX; arches 4 + 6 = X.
</Mnemonic>

### Pharyngeal pouches (endoderm)
| Pouch | Derivatives |
|---|---|
| **1** | Middle ear cavity + Eustachian tube + mastoid air cells |
| **2** | Palatine tonsils |
| **3** | **Inferior parathyroids + thymus** (note migration → ectopic thymic parathyroid) |
| **4** | **Superior parathyroids + ultimobranchial body → C cells** |

**DiGeorge** = 3rd + 4th pouch failure → no thymus + no parathyroids + cardiac outflow defects + cleft palate + hypocalcemia + T-cell deficiency.

### Gut tube - three regions
| Region | Foregut | Midgut | Hindgut |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Embryologic spans** | Pharynx → 2nd part of duodenum | 3rd part of duodenum → proximal 2/3 transverse colon | Distal 1/3 transverse → upper anal canal |
| **Artery** | Celiac (T12) | SMA (L1) | IMA (L3) |
| **Vagal innervation** | Yes (anterior + posterior vagal trunks) | Yes (posterior vagal trunk) | Pelvic splanchnic (S2–S4 parasympathetic) |
| **Parasympathetic sympathetic chain levels** | Greater splanchnic (T5–T9) | Lesser splanchnic (T10–T11) | Lumbar splanchnic + pelvic |

### Midgut rotation
- Week 6: midgut herniates into umbilical cord (rapid growth + small abdomen).
- Week 10: returns to abdomen + **rotates 270° counterclockwise** around the SMA axis.
- Failure → **omphalocele** (failure of return; covered by amnion) vs **gastroschisis** (defect of body wall; bowel exposed; right of umbilicus).
- Malrotation → predisposes to volvulus + obstruction.

### Genitourinary system
- **Pronephros** (week 4, nonfunctional) → **mesonephros** (functional in first trimester) → **metanephros** (definitive kidney). Kidneys ascend from pelvis to T12–L3.
- **Wolffian (mesonephric) ducts** → male: epididymis, vas deferens, seminal vesicle, ejaculatory duct ("**SEED**").
- **Müllerian (paramesonephric) ducts** → female: fallopian tubes, uterus, cervix, upper 1/3 vagina.
- Sex determination: **SRY** on Y chromosome → testis development → Sertoli cells make **Müllerian inhibiting substance (MIS)** → regress paramesonephric. Leydig cells make **testosterone** → develops Wolffian + **DHT** (via 5α-reductase) → external genitalia + prostate.
- Default sex = **female** (no SRY → no MIS → Müllerian persists; no testosterone → Wolffian regresses).

### Lung development - 5 stages
1. **Embryonic** (weeks 4–7) - lung bud from foregut endoderm.
2. **Pseudoglandular** (weeks 5–17) - branching; can't survive if born here.
3. **Canalicular** (weeks 16–25) - bronchioles + capillary growth.
4. **Saccular** (weeks 24–38) - terminal air sacs; **type II pneumocytes + surfactant by ~24 weeks** → viability threshold.
5. **Alveolar** (week 36–8 years post-natal) - mature alveoli; bulk of alveolar growth is postnatal.

## Common congenital malformations - summary table

| Malformation | Embryologic basis |
|---|---|
| **Spina bifida** | Failure of caudal neural tube closure (day 28); folate deficiency; ↑ AFP |
| **Anencephaly** | Failure of cranial neural tube closure (day 25); ↑↑ AFP |
| **Cleft lip** | Failure of fusion of medial nasal + maxillary prominences |
| **Cleft palate** | Failure of fusion of palatine shelves |
| **Tetralogy of Fallot** | Anterosuperior displacement of infundibular septum (neural crest) → VSD + overriding aorta + pulm stenosis + RVH |
| **Transposition of great arteries** | Aorticopulmonary septum fails to spiral |
| **Esophageal atresia + tracheoesophageal fistula** | Incomplete septation of foregut (90% have distal TEF with proximal blind esophagus) |
| **Duodenal atresia** | Failure of recanalization (week 8) - "double-bubble" on imaging; trisomy 21 |
| **Jejunal/ileal atresia** | Vascular accident in utero ("apple peel") |
| **Hirschsprung** | Failure of neural crest cell migration → no enteric ganglia in distal colon |
| **Diaphragmatic hernia** | Failure of pleuroperitoneal membrane closure (left posterolateral, Bochdalek) → abdominal contents in chest → pulmonary hypoplasia |
| **Bladder exstrophy** | Failure of anterior abdominal wall closure |
| **Hypospadias** | Failure of urethral fold fusion (ventral opening) |
| **Cryptorchidism** | Failure of testicular descent |
| **Patent urachus** | Open allantois → urine from umbilicus |
| **Meckel diverticulum** | Persistent vitelline (omphalomesenteric) duct - "**rule of 2s**": 2% prevalence, 2 feet from ileocecal valve, 2 inches long, 2 types of ectopic mucosa (gastric + pancreatic), presents before age 2 |
| **Persistent truncus arteriosus** | Failure of aorticopulmonary septation (neural crest) |
| **DiGeorge syndrome** | 22q11.2 deletion → failure of 3rd + 4th pouches |

## Function reference - every major principle

| Concept | Function | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Neural crest** | Migrates everywhere; multiple lineages | Defects cause: DiGeorge (pharyngeal arches), Hirschsprung (enteric), neurocristopathies, Waardenburg, neurofibromatosis |
| **Aorticopulmonary septum (NC origin)** | Splits truncus → aorta + PA | Failure: persistent truncus, TGA, TOF |
| **Mesonephric (Wolffian) duct** | Male internal genitalia | Persists if testosterone present |
| **Paramesonephric (Müllerian) duct** | Female internal genitalia | Persists if MIS absent |
| **Notochord** | Inducer of neural plate + nucleus pulposus in adult | Disc herniation: nucleus pulposus extruded |
| **SRY gene** | Testis-determining factor | Mutation → XY female; translocation → XX male |
| **Allantois → urachus** | Drains fetal bladder | Patent → umbilical urine; cyst → midline mass |
| **Vitelline duct** | Connects gut to yolk sac | Persists as Meckel diverticulum |
| **Septum transversum** | Becomes central tendon of diaphragm + ventral mesentery of stomach | Origin of liver between leaves |
| **Pleuroperitoneal membrane** | Separates pleural from peritoneal cavities | Failure → diaphragmatic hernia |
| **Cloacal membrane** | Closes hindgut + GU outlets | Imperforate anus, cloacal exstrophy |

## Why this matters

<HighYield variant="boards">
- **Tetralogy of Fallot (TOF)**: most common cyanotic congenital heart disease beyond infancy. **4 features**: pulmonary stenosis, RVH, VSD, overriding aorta. "Boot-shaped heart" on CXR. Squat → ↑ SVR → ↓ R-to-L shunt → relieves cyanosis.
- **Down syndrome (T21)** associations: endocardial cushion defects (AV canal), duodenal atresia, Hirschsprung, ALL, Alzheimer.
- **Edwards (T18)**: rocker-bottom feet, clenched fists, micrognathia, CHD; lethal.
- **Patau (T13)**: holoprosencephaly, polydactyly, cleft, microphthalmia; lethal.
- **DiGeorge**: 22q11.2; CATCH-22.
- **APGAR score** (1 + 5 min after birth): Appearance, Pulse, Grimace, Activity, Respiration (0–2 each, max 10).
- **Apgar &lt;7 at 5 min** → consider neonatal resuscitation issues + ↑ risk of cerebral palsy.
- **Triple/quad screen** (15–20 weeks): low AFP/uE3 + high hCG + high inhibin A = Down. High AFP = neural tube defect or abdominal wall defect.
- **Cell-free fetal DNA** (NIPT, week 10+): >99% sensitivity for trisomies. Replacing first-trimester combined screen.
- **Critical congenital heart defects** screened by **pulse oximetry** at 24–48 h of life (R hand + foot, looking for >3% difference or absolute SpO₂ &lt;95%).
</HighYield>

A **combining form** is the root + connecting vowel (usually /o). Some roots have multiple forms - the Greek + Latin both survive in modern medical English.

## Mnemonic - "system, root, organ"

**Cardio-** (heart) and **Vascul-** (vessel) for CV.
**Pulmon-/pneumo-** lungs, **rhino-/naso-** nose, **trachea-** windpipe.
**Gastro-** stomach, **entero-** intestine.
**Hepat-** liver, **cholecyst-** gallbladder, **pancreat-** pancreas.
**Nephr-/ren-** kidney; **ur-/urin-** urine; **cyst-** bladder.
**Orchi-/test-** testis; **prostat-** prostate.
**Ov-/oophor-** ovary; **salping-** tubes; **hyster-/metr-** uterus.
**Oste-** bone, **arthr-** joint, **my-/muscle-** muscle, **chondr-** cartilage.
**Neur-** nerve, **encephal-** brain, **mening-** meninges.
**Ophthalm-/ocul-** eye, **ot-/aur-** ear, **dermat-** skin.

<HighYield variant="boards">
- **myel/o** has TWO meanings - bone marrow OR spinal cord. Context decides.
- **cyst/o** means bladder usually, but also any "sac" (e.g., dacryocystitis = tear sac).
- **salping/o** is uterine tube AND Eustachian tube (both anatomically similar shapes).
- **vas/o** is vas deferens or vessel - context.
- **kerat/o** is cornea OR horny tissue (skin).
- "kal/i" = potassium (Latin **kalium**, K). "natr/i" = sodium (**natrium**, Na). Hence kalemia, natremia.
- Mast/o (Greek mastos) = breast for procedures (mastectomy); mamm/o = breast for imaging (mammogram).
- Oophor/o ovary (Greek) for surgery; ovari/o (Latin) for general use.
- Hyster/o = uterus for hysterectomy; metr/o = uterus for endometritis.
- Hepat/o + -itis = hepatitis (specific liver inflammation, often viral).
</HighYield>

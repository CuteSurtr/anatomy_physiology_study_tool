<HighYield variant="boards">
- Acronyms vary by specialty; many overlap (PVD = peripheral vascular / posterior vitreous; PCP = primary care provider, Pneumocystis pneumonia, phencyclidine).
- **GTPAL** for OB history.
- **ABCDE** = primary survey (trauma) + melanoma screen.
- **NIHSS** = stroke; **GCS** = consciousness; **APGAR** = neonate.
- **MME** monitoring matters with chronic opioid prescribing (CDC guidelines).
- "**Goals of care**" = patient-centered prognosis + values discussion.
- **DNR ≠ DNI** ≠ comfort-only - distinct decisions in the same conversation.
</HighYield>

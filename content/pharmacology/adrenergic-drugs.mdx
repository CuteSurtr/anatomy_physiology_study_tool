Adrenergic receptors are GPCRs distributed throughout the body. Different drugs preferentially activate different subtypes, producing characteristic clinical profiles.

## Receptors at a glance

| Receptor | G-protein | Tissue | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **α1** | Gq → IP3/DAG | Vascular smooth muscle, urinary sphincter, iris dilator, pilomotor | Vasoconstriction, mydriasis, ↑ BP |
| **α2** | Gi → ↓ cAMP | Presynaptic (CNS + sympathetic); pancreatic β-cells | ↓ NE release, ↓ insulin, ↓ sympathetic outflow |
| **β1** | Gs → ↑ cAMP | Heart, kidney (JG cells) | ↑ HR, ↑ contractility, ↑ renin |
| **β2** | Gs → ↑ cAMP | Bronchi, vascular smooth muscle (esp. skeletal m.), uterus, liver, pancreas | Bronchodilation, vasodilation, tocolytic, glycogenolysis, ↑ insulin |
| **β3** | Gs | Adipose, detrusor | Lipolysis, bladder relaxation |
| **D1** | Gs | Renal, mesenteric, coronary vessels | Vasodilation (fenoldopam) |

## Direct agonists by receptor profile

| Drug | Receptors | Clinical use |
|---|---|---|
| **Phenylephrine** | α1 only | Decongestant, mydriasis, hypotension during anesthesia (no tachycardia) |
| **Methoxamine** | α1 | Hypotension (historical) |
| **Norepinephrine** | α1 = α2 = β1 (NO β2) | Septic shock pressor; vasoconstrictor of choice |
| **Epinephrine** | α + β (high dose α dominates, low dose β dominates) | Anaphylaxis, cardiac arrest (ACLS), local anesthetic adjunct, croup |
| **Dopamine** | D1 (low) → β1 (mid) → α1 (high), dose-dependent | Shock; low dose "renal" perfusion (controversial) |
| **Dobutamine** | β1 >> β2 (β1-selective) | Cardiogenic shock, heart failure inotropy |
| **Isoproterenol** | β1 = β2 (no α) | Bradycardia (rarely used now); torsades stabilization |
| **Albuterol, terbutaline** | β2 selective (short-acting) | Acute asthma; terbutaline also tocolytic |
| **Salmeterol, formoterol** | β2 selective (long-acting) | Asthma + COPD maintenance (paired with ICS) |
| **Clonidine, α-methyldopa** | α2 central agonists | HTN (esp. in pregnancy for α-methyldopa); opioid + alcohol withdrawal; ADHD |
| **Dexmedetomidine** | α2 (potent, sedating) | ICU sedation; intraoperative |
| **Fenoldopam** | D1 | Hypertensive crisis (preserves renal perfusion) |

## Indirect-acting sympathomimetics

These work by ↑ release or ↓ reuptake of endogenous NE:

| Drug | Mechanism | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Ephedrine** | Releases NE; weak direct α/β | Hypotension during anesthesia |
| **Pseudoephedrine** | Releases NE; oral decongestant | Decongestant (behind the counter due to meth precursor) |
| **Phenylpropanolamine** | Withdrawn (hemorrhagic stroke risk) | (historic) appetite suppressant |
| **Amphetamine** | Releases NE + DA | ADHD (Adderall), narcolepsy (modafinil alternative) |
| **Methamphetamine** | Stronger CNS release | Recreational/illegal; "ice" |
| **MDMA** | Releases serotonin + DA + NE | Recreational; serotonin syndrome risk |
| **Cocaine** | Blocks NE/DA/5-HT reuptake; local anesthetic (Na+ block) | Local anesthetic in ENT; recreational |
| **Tyramine** | Releases NE (food + MAOI = HTN crisis) | Aged cheese, wine - dangerous with MAOIs |

## Catecholamine pressor matrix - high yield

| Drug | HR | Contractility | SVR | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Phenylephrine** | ↓ (reflex) | – | ↑↑ | Pure α; reflex brady |
| **Norepinephrine** | – | ↑ | ↑↑ | First-line septic shock |
| **Epinephrine (low dose)** | ↑ | ↑ | ↓ (β2) | Anaphylaxis low dose, septic shock |
| **Epinephrine (high dose)** | ↑↑ | ↑↑ | ↑ | Cardiac arrest |
| **Dopamine (mid)** | ↑↑ | ↑ | ↑ (mixed) | Cardiogenic shock |
| **Dobutamine** | ↑ | ↑↑ | – or ↓ | Cardiogenic shock + HF |
| **Isoproterenol** | ↑↑ | ↑↑ | ↓ | Pure β; orthostatic |

## Side effects + warnings

- **All sympathomimetics** can cause tachyarrhythmia, hypertension (overshoot), tremor, anxiety, hyperglycemia.
- **β2 agonists** in asthma: ↓ K⁺ (shifts intracellularly - useful in hyperkalemia), tremor, tachycardia, tolerance with chronic use. Long-acting β2 alone (e.g., salmeterol monotherapy) ↑ asthma mortality - MUST pair with ICS.
- **Cocaine** chest pain: avoid β-blockers (unopposed α → worsening vasospasm). Use benzodiazepines + nitroglycerin + CCB.
- **Pheochromocytoma** crisis: hypertension from catecholamine surge. Treat with α-block (phenoxybenzamine) FIRST, then β-block. Never β alone (unopposed α).
- **Pregnant + asthma**: terbutaline + albuterol safe. Avoid epinephrine if possible (placental constriction).

<Clinical>
**Anaphylaxis** management - epinephrine **IM** 0.3–0.5 mg in adult thigh, repeat q5–15 min PRN. Mechanism: α1 (vasoconstriction → reverses hypotension + airway edema), β1 (↑ HR, contractility), β2 (bronchodilation, ↓ histamine release from mast cells). Adjuncts: H1 antagonist, H2 antagonist, glucocorticoid (delayed onset, prevents biphasic reaction).

**Septic shock vasopressor cascade**: fluids → norepinephrine first → add vasopressin (V1) or epinephrine if MAP still &lt;65 → consider dobutamine if low CO + adequate MAP. Hydrocortisone if pressor-refractory.

**Cardiogenic shock**: dobutamine + norepinephrine; consider milrinone (PDE3 inhibitor, lusitropy + vasodilation) if not adrenergic-receptor responsive. Mechanical (Impella, balloon pump) if drugs fail.

**ADHD pharmacotherapy**: methylphenidate (Ritalin) - blocks DA/NE reuptake; amphetamine (Adderall) - releases DA/NE. Both raise pulse + BP modestly. Atomoxetine is non-stimulant (selective NRI). Black box: SCD in undiagnosed structural heart disease.
</Clinical>

<HighYield variant="boards">
- "Pure α1 agonist" (phenylephrine) on a patient: ↑ BP + ↓ HR (reflex from baroreceptors). Atropine **un-blocks** the reflex → ↑ BP **without** the bradycardia.
- "Pure β agonist" (isoproterenol): ↑ HR + ↑ contractility + ↓ DBP + widened pulse pressure.
- Epinephrine reversal: in a normal subject epi raises BP; in a subject on an α-blocker (e.g., phentolamine), epi LOWERS BP because β2 vasodilation is unopposed.
- Clonidine withdrawal → rebound hypertension. Taper gradually.
- α-methyldopa = HTN of choice in pregnancy.
- Pseudoephedrine + MAOIs = hypertensive crisis.
- Cocaine MI: AVOID β-blockers. Use benzodiazepines.
- Pheo: α-block (phenoxybenzamine) BEFORE β-block.
- Salmeterol + ICS or formoterol + ICS, never LABA alone in asthma.
</HighYield>

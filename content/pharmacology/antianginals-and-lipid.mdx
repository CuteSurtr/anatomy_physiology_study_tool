## Antianginal drugs

Three goals in stable angina:
1. ↓ myocardial O₂ demand (HR, contractility, wall stress).
2. ↑ myocardial O₂ supply (coronary vasodilation).
3. Prevent platelet/clot events (aspirin, statin).

### Nitrates

**Mechanism**: nitrate → NO → ↑ cGMP → smooth muscle relaxation. Predominantly **venodilation** (↓ preload → ↓ wall stress + ↓ O₂ demand). Coronary dilation also.

| Drug | Route | Duration | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nitroglycerin SL** | Tablet/spray | 5–15 min | Acute anginal episode |
| **Nitroglycerin IV** | Continuous | titratable | Unstable angina, HTN, HF |
| **Isosorbide dinitrate** | PO TID | 6–8 h | Chronic angina, HF (with hydralazine) |
| **Isosorbide mononitrate** | PO daily | 12 h | Chronic angina |
| **Nitroglycerin patch** | Transdermal 12 h on, 12 h off | | Tolerance prevention - asymmetric dosing |

**Side effects**: headache (NO vasodilation - common dose-limiting), flushing, hypotension, reflex tachycardia.

**Contraindications**:
- **Phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors** (sildenafil, tadalafil) within 24–48 h - severe hypotension.
- Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (preload reduction worsens outflow obstruction).
- Right ventricular infarction (preload-dependent).
- Severe AS.

**Nitrate tolerance**: 24 h continuous → tolerance. Always need an **8–12 h nitrate-free interval** daily.

### β-blockers

Reduce HR + contractility + wall stress → ↓ O₂ demand. Mortality benefit post-MI. See [adrenergic antagonists](/pharmacology/adrenergic-antagonists).

### CCBs

- **Non-DHP (verapamil, diltiazem)** - rate control + coronary vasodilation. Useful when β-blocker contraindicated.
- **DHP (amlodipine)** - peripheral + coronary vasodilation. **Vasospastic (Prinzmetal) angina** - first line.

### Ranolazine

**Mechanism**: blocks late Na current → ↓ Na-induced Ca overload → ↓ diastolic wall stress.

**Use**: chronic angina refractory to standard therapy. Doesn't affect HR/BP.

**Side effects**: QT prolongation, constipation, dizziness, nausea.

**Drug interactions**: CYP3A4 substrate - avoid with strong inhibitors (ketoconazole).

### Ivabradine

If channel blocker → ↓ SA node firing. Adjunctive for chronic angina with normal sinus rhythm + HR ≥70 despite max β-blocker.

## ACS pharmacology summary

**STEMI / NSTEMI** acute management:
- **Aspirin** 162–325 mg chewed.
- **P2Y12 inhibitor** (ticagrelor, prasugrel, clopidogrel) loading.
- **Anticoagulation** (heparin, enoxaparin, bivalirudin, fondaparinux).
- **β-blocker** if no contraindication (within 24 h, oral).
- **High-intensity statin** (atorvastatin 80 mg).
- **ACE-i** (especially if LV dysfunction, anterior MI).
- **Nitroglycerin** for ischemic pain.
- **Morphine** only if pain refractory (concerns about absorption interference with P2Y12).
- **PCI within 90 min (STEMI)** or risk-stratified (NSTEMI).

After discharge - "ABCDE": Aspirin + P2Y12 (DAPT), β-blocker, Cholesterol drug (statin), Diet/Diabetes/Don't smoke, Exercise.

## Lipid-lowering drugs

### Statins (HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors)

**Mechanism**: block hepatic cholesterol synthesis → ↑ LDL receptor expression → ↓ serum LDL.

| Drug | Intensity | t½ |
|---|---|---|
| **Atorvastatin 40-80 mg** | High | 14 h |
| **Rosuvastatin 20-40 mg** | High | 19 h |
| **Simvastatin 20-40 mg** | Moderate | 2 h |
| **Pravastatin 40 mg** | Moderate | 2 h - water-soluble; less drug-interaction risk |
| **Pitavastatin 4 mg** | Moderate | 12 h - less CYP3A4 interaction |
| **Fluvastatin** | Moderate | 1 h - rarely used |
| **Lovastatin** | Low | 3 h |

**High-intensity statin** lowers LDL ≥50%. **Moderate** lowers 30–50%.

**Indications** (ACC/AHA 2018):
1. Clinical ASCVD - high-intensity (≤75 yrs); moderate (>75).
2. LDL ≥190 - high-intensity.
3. Diabetic age 40–75 - moderate to high.
4. Primary prevention age 40–75 with ASCVD 10-yr risk ≥7.5% - moderate to high.

**Side effects**:
- **Myopathy** - myalgia common; rhabdomyolysis rare. Risk ↑ with: gemfibrozil, niacin, CYP3A4 inhibitors (azoles, macrolides, cyclosporine, grapefruit juice), Asian descent for rosuvastatin.
- **Transaminitis** - mild, common; &lt;1% true hepatitis. Check baseline LFT; routine monitoring not required.
- **New-onset diabetes** - small ↑ risk (~9% over 4 yrs); benefit outweighs.
- **Cognitive** - rare; FDA label.
- **Teratogenic** (category X) - hold in pregnancy.

**Drug-specific points**:
- Atorvastatin - extensively metabolized 3A4 (interactions).
- Rosuvastatin - mostly unchanged in urine; renally dose-adjust.
- Pravastatin - hydrophilic, minimal CYP - good for HIV + transplant patients.
- Simvastatin 80 mg restricted (myopathy).

### Ezetimibe

**Mechanism**: blocks intestinal cholesterol absorption (NPC1L1 transporter).

**Effect**: ↓ LDL ~15–20%. Synergistic with statin.

**IMPROVE-IT**: adding ezetimibe to simvastatin reduces cardiovascular events post-ACS. First non-statin to show benefit.

**Side effects**: rare myopathy, GI upset.

### PCSK9 inhibitors

**Alirocumab, Evolocumab** - monoclonal antibodies that bind PCSK9 → ↓ degradation of LDL receptor → more LDL receptors → ↓ LDL ~60%.

**Use**: ASCVD + LDL not controlled on max statin + ezetimibe; familial hypercholesterolemia.

**Inclisiran** - siRNA that silences PCSK9 mRNA. Same effect, given subcut every **6 months**.

**Side effects**: injection site reactions, flu-like symptoms. Cost was very high; coming down.

### Bempedoic acid

**Mechanism**: ATP citrate lyase inhibitor (upstream of HMG-CoA reductase) → ↓ cholesterol synthesis. Prodrug activated only in liver → no muscle ADP citrate lyase → **no myopathy**.

**Use**: statin-intolerant patients; add-on for residual LDL.

**Side effects**: ↑ uric acid (→ gout), tendon rupture rare.

### Fibrates

**Fenofibrate, Gemfibrozil** - PPAR-α agonists → ↑ LPL activity, ↑ HDL, ↓ TG.

**Use**: severe hypertriglyceridemia (>500 mg/dL - pancreatitis prevention).

**Side effects**: gallstones (cholestasis), myopathy (esp. **gemfibrozil + statin** - avoid combination; fenofibrate safer), ↑ LFT, ↑ creatinine.

### Niacin (nicotinic acid)

**Mechanism**: ↓ hepatic VLDL secretion → ↓ LDL + ↓ TG; ↑ HDL most of any drug class.

**Use**: largely abandoned for CV prevention (AIM-HIGH, HPS2-THRIVE negative). Still used for refractory low HDL or familial hyperlipidemia.

**Side effects**: **flushing** (PGD2-mediated; pretreat with aspirin), hyperglycemia, hyperuricemia, hepatotoxicity (esp. SR formulation), nausea, pruritus.

### Bile acid sequestrants

**Cholestyramine, Colesevelam, Colestipol**

**Mechanism**: bind bile acids in gut → ↑ hepatic LDL receptor activity to make more bile acids → ↓ LDL.

**Use**: hypercholesterolemia in pregnancy (not absorbed); pruritus from biliary obstruction (binds bile salts); adjunct in chronic diarrhea due to bile acid malabsorption.

**Side effects**: GI upset, bloating, ↓ absorption of fat-soluble vitamins + many drugs (digoxin, warfarin, thiazides, thyroxine - dose 4 h apart).

### Omega-3 / Icosapent ethyl

**Mechanism**: ↓ hepatic VLDL synthesis, anti-inflammatory.

**Icosapent ethyl (Vascepa, EPA-only)** - **REDUCE-IT trial**: 25% CV event reduction in patients with TG 135–500 on statin therapy. The mixed EPA+DHA formulations (Lovaza) did NOT show benefit (STRENGTH).

**Side effects**: A-fib, bleeding tendency.

## Triglyceride-focused algorithm

| TG level | Approach |
|---|---|
| &lt;500 mg/dL | Lifestyle, statin (treats LDL + modest TG benefit) |
| >500 mg/dL | Add fibrate or icosapent ethyl to prevent pancreatitis |
| >1000 mg/dL | Hospitalize - IV insulin, NPO, plasmapheresis for refractory |

## Special situations

- **Familial hypercholesterolemia (FH)**: high-intensity statin + ezetimibe + PCSK9 inhibitor. Cascade screen relatives.
- **Pregnancy + high LDL**: bile acid sequestrants only (not absorbed). Stop statin before conception.
- **Liver disease**: monitor LFTs more closely; pravastatin or pitavastatin safer (less hepatic metabolism). Don't use in cirrhosis Child C.
- **Renal disease**: dose-adjust rosuvastatin, simvastatin. Statins still beneficial in CKD (not in dialysis though - 4D, AURORA negative).

<Clinical>
**Statin-associated muscle symptoms (SAMS)**: common - 5–10% of patients. Real myopathy with CK > 10× ULN < 1%. Strategy: stop, recheck CK, restart different statin at lower dose, consider every-other-day rosuvastatin/atorvastatin. Persistent intolerance → bempedoic acid + ezetimibe + PCSK9 inhibitor combinations.

**Acute pancreatitis with TG >1000**: NPO, IV fluids, IV insulin to drive TG into adipocytes, plasmapheresis for refractory or severe. Discharge plan = fenofibrate or icosapent + statin + alcohol abstinence + diabetes control.

**Prinzmetal/vasospastic angina**: **CCB first line** (amlodipine, diltiazem). Avoid β-blocker (can worsen vasospasm by unopposed α). Add nitrates for breakthrough.

**Post-MI medication bundle** ("ABCDE"): Aspirin + P2Y12, β-blocker, Cholesterol (high-intensity statin), Diet/Diabetes/Don't smoke, Exercise + ACE-i if EF reduced.
</Clinical>

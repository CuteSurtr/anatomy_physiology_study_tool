## Herpes virus drugs

### Acyclovir family

**Acyclovir, Valacyclovir, Famciclovir**

**Mechanism**: prodrug → activated by viral thymidine kinase (in HSV/VZV-infected cells) → triphosphate → chain-terminating DNA polymerase substrate. **Selective for HSV, VZV** (less for CMV - no TK).

**Drugs**:
- **Acyclovir** - IV/PO/topical. PO bioavailability ~20%.
- **Valacyclovir** - prodrug of acyclovir; better PO bioavailability (~55%).
- **Famciclovir** - prodrug of penciclovir; HSV/VZV.

**Uses**: genital + orolabial HSV, HSV encephalitis (IV acyclovir), VZV (chickenpox, shingles).

**Side effects**:
- **Crystalluria + AKI** (esp. IV with poor hydration) - **always hydrate** before/during IV.
- Neurotoxicity (myoclonus, encephalopathy, especially renal failure).
- Hepatotoxicity (rare).

### Ganciclovir family

**Ganciclovir, Valganciclovir**

**Mechanism**: prodrug → activated by CMV UL97 kinase → triphosphate → CMV DNA polymerase substrate.

**Use**: **CMV** disease (retinitis, colitis, pneumonitis, encephalitis), prophylaxis in transplant.

**Side effects**:
- **Marrow suppression** (neutropenia worst) - check ANC; G-CSF support.
- Renal toxicity.
- Teratogenic - contraception both partners.

**Valganciclovir** = PO prodrug with much better bioavailability than oral ganciclovir.

### Foscarnet

**Mechanism**: pyrophosphate analog; **doesn't require activation** (no kinase). Inhibits viral DNA polymerase.

**Use**: ganciclovir-resistant CMV, acyclovir-resistant HSV/VZV.

**Side effects**: **nephrotoxicity** (serious), electrolyte abnormalities (↓ Ca, K, Mg, PO₄), seizures, genital ulcers.

### Cidofovir

**Mechanism**: nucleotide analog (already monophosphorylated → cell adds remaining phosphates).

**Use**: CMV retinitis, BK virus, adenovirus (alternative).

**Side effects**: severe **nephrotoxicity** (Fanconi-like, pre-treat with IV NS + **probenecid** before each dose), neutropenia, ocular hypotony.

### Letermovir

**Mechanism**: blocks CMV terminase complex (different mechanism).

**Use**: **CMV prophylaxis post-allogeneic HSCT** (very effective).

**Side effects**: well-tolerated; CYP3A4 substrate + inhibitor.

### Maribavir

**Mechanism**: inhibits CMV UL97 kinase.

**Use**: refractory CMV in transplant.

## Influenza drugs

### Neuraminidase inhibitors

| Drug | Route | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Oseltamivir** (Tamiflu) | PO | Influenza A + B treatment + prophylaxis |
| **Zanamivir** | Inhaled | Avoid in asthma (bronchospasm) |
| **Peramivir** | IV | Hospitalized severe |

**Use**: within 48 h of symptom onset for outpatient; consider beyond 48 h for hospitalized.

**Side effects**: GI (oseltamivir nausea, vomiting), bronchospasm (zanamivir). Rare neuropsychiatric (especially adolescents).

### Cap-dependent endonuclease inhibitor

**Baloxavir marboxil (Xofluza)** - **single dose** PO; influenza A + B. Faster symptom resolution than oseltamivir in some trials.

### M2 inhibitors

**Amantadine, Rimantadine** - block M2 ion channel of influenza A. **Resistance widespread** - **no longer recommended**.

## Hepatitis B

| Drug | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Entecavir** | High barrier to resistance; preferred 1st line if naive |
| **Tenofovir disoproxil (TDF)** | Active vs HBV + HIV; renal + bone toxicity with long use |
| **Tenofovir alafenamide (TAF)** | Safer renal + bone profile |
| **Adefovir** | Largely replaced (renal toxicity, weak) |
| **Lamivudine** | Largely replaced (resistance) |
| **Telbivudine** | Replaced |
| **Pegylated interferon-α** | Time-limited course (48 weeks); flu-like + cytopenias + autoimmune; less common now |

**Goal**: viral suppression + ALT normalization. Functional cure (HBsAg loss) uncommon.

## Hepatitis C

Modern era = **direct-acting antivirals (DAAs)**, 95%+ SVR (cure).

| Drug | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sofosbuvir** | NS5B polymerase | Backbone of most regimens |
| **Ledipasvir, Velpatasvir, Pibrentasvir** | NS5A inhibitors | Always combined |
| **Glecaprevir, Voxilaprevir** | NS3/4A protease | Latest combos |

**Common regimens**:
- **Glecaprevir + pibrentasvir** (Mavyret) - 8 weeks for treatment-naive; pan-genotypic.
- **Sofosbuvir + velpatasvir** (Epclusa) - 12 weeks; pan-genotypic.
- **Sofosbuvir + velpatasvir + voxilaprevir** (Vosevi) - retreatment after DAA failure.

**Side effects**: well-tolerated (fatigue, headache). **Reactivation of HBV** in HBV-coinfected → screen + treat HBV.

**Ribavirin** still used in some niches (HCV cirrhosis + decompensated, HEV, RSV) - **hemolytic anemia**, teratogen (washout 6 months).

## HIV - antiretroviral therapy (ART)

**Always combination therapy** (3 drugs typically). Goals: undetectable viral load, immune restoration. **U=U (Undetectable = Untransmissible)**.

### Classes

| Class | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **NRTI** (nucleoside RT inhibitors) | Tenofovir, Emtricitabine, Lamivudine, Abacavir, Zidovudine | Chain terminators; mitochondrial toxicity (lactic acidosis, lipoatrophy with old drugs) |
| **NNRTI** | Efavirenz, Rilpivirine, Doravirine, Nevirapine, Etravirine | Bind RT allosterically; CYP interactions; rash |
| **PI** (protease inhibitor) | Darunavir, Atazanavir, Lopinavir (always boosted with ritonavir or cobicistat) | Lipid + metabolic side effects; many interactions |
| **INSTI** (integrase strand transfer inhibitor) | Raltegravir, Dolutegravir, Bictegravir, Cabotegravir, Elvitegravir | Best tolerated; preferred 1st line; ↑ weight, headache |
| **Entry inhibitor** | Maraviroc (CCR5 - need tropism test); Enfuvirtide (gp41 fusion - SC injection) | Salvage therapy |
| **CD4-directed** | Ibalizumab (monoclonal Ab vs CD4) | IV q2 weeks - salvage |
| **Capsid inhibitor** | Lenacapavir | Long-acting SC q6 months - salvage + PrEP studies |
| **Attachment inhibitor** | Fostemsavir | Multi-drug-resistant HIV |

### First-line regimens (DHHS 2024)

**Single tablet, once daily, INSTI-based**:
- **Bictegravir / tenofovir alafenamide / emtricitabine** (Biktarvy) - workhorse.
- **Dolutegravir / abacavir / lamivudine** (Triumeq) - check HLA-B*5701 before abacavir.
- **Dolutegravir + TAF + emtricitabine** (combined regimens vary).
- **Dolutegravir + lamivudine** (Dovato) - 2-drug regimen; cure-naive virologically suppressed.

### Drug-specific notes

- **Tenofovir disoproxil (TDF)** - renal + bone toxicity. **Tenofovir alafenamide (TAF)** safer.
- **Abacavir** - **HLA-B*5701** screen before starting (severe hypersensitivity reaction).
- **Efavirenz** - vivid dreams, dizziness, depression, teratogen (NTD - now considered acceptable in pregnancy per WHO); take at bedtime.
- **Rilpivirine** - needs food + acidic stomach; QT.
- **Nevirapine** - hepatotoxicity (women with CD4 >250 highest risk), rash → SJS.
- **Atazanavir** - **indirect hyperbilirubinemia** (Gilbert-like - inhibits UGT) - cosmetic only.
- **Dolutegravir** - mild weight gain; NTD signal initially → now considered low risk; can use in pregnancy.
- **Maraviroc** - need CCR5 tropism testing (Trofile).
- **Cabotegravir + rilpivirine** - **long-acting injectable** monthly or q2 months for maintenance.

### PrEP (pre-exposure prophylaxis)

- **Tenofovir DF / emtricitabine** (Truvada) - daily oral; the original.
- **Tenofovir alafenamide / emtricitabine** (Descovy) - daily oral; for men + transgender women (not vaginal exposure data).
- **Cabotegravir** (Apretude) - IM **q2 months** injectable PrEP - now an option.

### PEP (post-exposure prophylaxis)

After exposure (needlestick, sexual assault): start within 72 h; 3-drug regimen × 28 days. Typical: **tenofovir + emtricitabine + dolutegravir** or raltegravir.

### IRIS

Immune reconstitution → paradoxical worsening of opportunistic infection or new infection (TB, MAC, CMV, cryptococcus, PCP). Don't stop ART; treat the OI.

## SARS-CoV-2 (COVID-19)

| Drug | Mechanism | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Paxlovid (Nirmatrelvir + Ritonavir)** | 3CL protease inhibitor + boost | Outpatient high-risk; within 5 days; massive CYP interactions |
| **Remdesivir** | RdRp nucleotide analog | IV 3-day outpatient (high-risk); 5-day inpatient |
| **Molnupiravir** | RdRp mutagenic nucleoside | Less effective; reserved |
| **Tixagevimab-cilgavimab** (Evusheld) | Monoclonal Ab | PrEP (withdrawn 2023 as resistance rose) |
| **Sotrovimab, Bebtelovimab** | Monoclonal Ab | Withdrawn 2022-23 |
| **Dexamethasone** | Anti-inflammatory | Hospitalized + supplemental O₂ |
| **Tocilizumab, Baricitinib** | IL-6 receptor / JAK inhibitor | Hospitalized with rising O₂ need |
| **Anticoagulation** | LMWH prophylactic | All hospitalized; therapeutic if O₂ but not ICU |

## RSV

**Palivizumab** - monoclonal Ab vs RSV F protein - **prophylaxis** in high-risk infants (prematurity, BPD, CHD). Monthly IM during season.

**Nirsevimab** - newer single-dose seasonal RSV prophylaxis for ALL infants (replaces palivizumab).

**RSV vaccine** (Arexvy, Abrysvo) - now approved for adults ≥60 and pregnant 32-36 weeks.

**Ribavirin** (aerosolized) - severe RSV pneumonia; rarely used.

## Hepatitis-treatment interactions to know

- **HBV reactivation** with rituximab, cancer chemo, immunosuppression - screen + prophylax with entecavir/TDF/TAF.
- **HIV/HCV coinfection**: treat HCV with DAA; some drug interactions (sofosbuvir + amiodarone → severe bradycardia).
- **HIV/HBV coinfection**: include TDF or TAF in ART (treats both); avoid lamivudine alone (resistance).

## "Drug of choice" matrix

| Virus | First-line |
|---|---|
| HSV (orolabial, genital) | Valacyclovir or acyclovir or famciclovir |
| HSV encephalitis | IV acyclovir |
| VZV (shingles, varicella) | Valacyclovir (within 72 h) |
| Acyclovir-resistant HSV | Foscarnet |
| CMV (retinitis, colitis, pneumonitis) | Ganciclovir / Valganciclovir |
| Ganciclovir-resistant CMV | Foscarnet, maribavir |
| CMV prophylaxis HSCT | Letermovir |
| Influenza | Oseltamivir or baloxavir |
| Hep B | Entecavir or TAF |
| Hep C | Glecaprevir-pibrentasvir or sofosbuvir-velpatasvir |
| HIV initial | Bictegravir/TAF/FTC or dolutegravir-based |
| COVID-19 high-risk outpatient | Paxlovid; remdesivir alternative |
| COVID-19 hospitalized | Dex ± remdesivir ± toci |
| RSV infant prophylaxis | Nirsevimab (or palivizumab) |

<Clinical>
**HSV encephalitis** - IV acyclovir 10 mg/kg q8h × 14-21 days. Don't wait for PCR confirmation if clinical suspicion is high - hydrate well.

**CMV retinitis in HIV (CD4 &lt;50)**: IV ganciclovir induction → PO valganciclovir maintenance; or intravitreal injection; start ART. Foscarnet if ganciclovir-resistant.

**Newly diagnosed HIV with CD4 50**: start ART (bictegravir-based) plus OI prophylaxis (TMP-SMX for PCP, azithromycin if CD4 &lt;50 for MAC). Screen TB, syphilis, HBV, HCV. Watch for IRIS.

**Pregnant patient with HIV**: continue ART; aim for undetectable VL by delivery. Most regimens safe; dolutegravir now considered safe. Intrapartum zidovudine if VL >50. Avoid breastfeeding in US (resource-rich); supported elsewhere.

**Paxlovid drug interactions**: ritonavir is a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor. Hold/adjust: statins, BZD, immunosuppressants, anticoagulants, amiodarone, etc. Review every med before prescribing.
</Clinical>

## Alkylating agents

**Mechanism**: covalently link to DNA → cross-links → can't replicate → apoptosis.

| Drug | Use | Special toxicity |
|---|---|---|
| **Cyclophosphamide** | Lymphoma, breast, autoimmune, transplant | **Hemorrhagic cystitis** (give **mesna** + IV NS); marrow; **SIADH**; secondary leukemia |
| **Ifosfamide** | Sarcoma, testicular | Same + **encephalopathy** (give **methylene blue**); hemorrhagic cystitis worse → MUST give mesna |
| **Mechlorethamine, melphalan, chlorambucil** | Hodgkin (MOPP), MM, CLL | Marrow suppression; secondary AML |
| **Bendamustine** | CLL, indolent lymphoma | Tolerated |
| **Carmustine (BCNU), Lomustine (CCNU)** | Brain tumors (cross BBB) | Pulmonary fibrosis (BCNU) |
| **Busulfan** | CML (historical), HSCT conditioning | **Pulmonary fibrosis**, hyperpigmentation, seizures |
| **Procarbazine** | Hodgkin (MOPP) | MAOI activity (tyramine reaction); disulfiram-like |
| **Streptozotocin** | Insulinoma | Nephrotoxicity |
| **Temozolomide** | Glioblastoma | Methylates DNA at O6 of guanine |
| **Dacarbazine (DTIC)** | Melanoma, sarcoma | N-V |

### Platinum compounds (platinum-DNA adducts)

| Drug | Use | Toxicity |
|---|---|---|
| **Cisplatin** | Testicular (cure!), lung, ovarian, bladder, head/neck | **Nephrotoxicity** (Mg wasting; pre-hydrate + amifostine), **ototoxicity** (high-frequency hearing loss), **peripheral neuropathy**, **severe N/V** (use 5-HT3 + NK1 + dex) |
| **Carboplatin** | Same indications; less nephrotoxic | **Marrow suppression** (thrombocytopenia worst) |
| **Oxaliplatin** | Colorectal | **Cold-induced peripheral neuropathy** (acute on cold drinks/air) |

## Antimetabolites

### Methotrexate (MTX)

**Mechanism**: dihydrofolate reductase inhibitor → ↓ THF → ↓ DNA/RNA + protein synthesis. Cell-cycle (S) phase specific.

**Use**: leukemia (ALL maintenance), lymphoma, choriocarcinoma, breast, head/neck; **rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, IBD** at low dose.

**Side effects**:
- **Marrow suppression** - rescue with **leucovorin** (folinic acid) at 24 h.
- **Hepatotoxicity** (fibrosis with chronic) - alcohol abstinence + LFT monitoring.
- **Pulmonary toxicity** (interstitial pneumonitis).
- **Renal tubular damage** at high dose (give IV NS + alkalinize urine).
- **Mucositis, oral ulcers** (give folic acid weekly to mitigate).
- **Teratogenic** (abortifacient at low dose; embryotoxic).

### Pemetrexed

Multi-targeted antifolate (DHFR + TS + GARFT). Use: mesothelioma, lung adenocarcinoma. Pretreat with folate + B12 + dex (↓ toxicity).

### Pyrimidine antagonists

| Drug | Mechanism | Use | Toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5-Fluorouracil (5-FU)** | Thymidylate synthase inhibitor | Colorectal, breast, head/neck, topical for actinic keratosis | Mucositis, diarrhea, hand-foot syndrome, coronary vasospasm; **leucovorin enhances** (not rescues) |
| **Capecitabine** | Oral 5-FU prodrug | Colorectal, breast | Hand-foot syndrome dominant |
| **Cytarabine (Ara-C)** | DNA polymerase inhibitor | AML, ALL | Marrow suppression; **cerebellar** + ocular at high dose; conjunctivitis (give dex drops) |
| **Gemcitabine** | DNA chain terminator | Pancreas, NSCLC, bladder, ovarian | Flu-like; cytopenias; HUS rare |

### Purine antagonists

| Drug | Mechanism | Use | Toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6-Mercaptopurine (6-MP)** | Purine analog; HGPRT activates | ALL maintenance, IBD | **Allopurinol/febuxostat ↑ levels** (block xanthine oxidase) - dose-adjust |
| **6-Thioguanine** | Similar | AML | |
| **Azathioprine** | Prodrug of 6-MP | Autoimmune, transplant | Same caveat with allopurinol |
| **Fludarabine** | Adenosine analog | CLL | Severe + prolonged lymphopenia → opportunistic infections |
| **Cladribine** | | Hairy cell leukemia | Single course often curative |
| **Clofarabine** | | Pediatric ALL | |

### Hydroxyurea

Ribonucleotide reductase inhibitor → ↓ dNTPs.

Uses: CML (historical), polycythemia vera, essential thrombocythemia, sickle cell disease (↑ HbF).

Side effects: marrow suppression, dermatologic, megaloblastosis.

## Antitumor antibiotics

| Drug | Use | Toxicity |
|---|---|---|
| **Doxorubicin (Adriamycin)** | Breast, lymphoma, sarcoma, AML | **Dose-related dilated cardiomyopathy** (cumulative >550 mg/m²); **dexrazoxane** protects |
| **Daunorubicin / Epirubicin / Idarubicin** | AML; breast | Same cardiac signal |
| **Bleomycin** | Hodgkin (ABVD), testicular | **Pulmonary fibrosis** (dose-related, cumulative >400 U); minimal myelosuppression |
| **Dactinomycin** | Wilms, sarcoma, gestational tumors | Mucositis, marrow |
| **Mitomycin C** | Anal, GI, bladder intravesical | Renal (HUS) |
| **Mitoxantrone** | AML, prostate, MS | Cardiomyopathy; blue urine |

**Anthracycline** mechanism = intercalation + topoisomerase II + free radical generation (mediates cardiac toxicity).

## Microtubule poisons

### Vinca alkaloids

**Vincristine, Vinblastine** - **bind tubulin → prevent assembly → block mitosis**.

| Drug | Use | Toxicity |
|---|---|---|
| **Vincristine** | ALL, lymphoma | **Peripheral neuropathy** (autonomic + sensory + motor); constipation; minimal marrow |
| **Vinblastine** | Hodgkin (ABVD), testicular | **Marrow suppression** (worse than vincristine); neuropathy less |
| **Vinorelbine** | NSCLC, breast | Mixed |

### Taxanes

**Paclitaxel, Docetaxel, Nab-paclitaxel, Cabazitaxel** - **stabilize microtubules** (opposite of vincas; prevents disassembly).

Use: ovarian, breast, lung, prostate.

Side effects:
- Hypersensitivity (paclitaxel - Cremophor vehicle - pretreat with dex, H1, H2; nab-paclitaxel avoids this).
- Marrow suppression.
- Peripheral neuropathy.
- Alopecia.
- Fluid retention (docetaxel).

### Other microtubule

- **Eribulin** - breast, liposarcoma.
- **Ixabepilone** - breast.
- **Plinabulin** - investigational.

## Topoisomerase inhibitors

### Topoisomerase II inhibitors

**Etoposide (VP-16), Teniposide** - small cell lung cancer, testicular, lymphoma, leukemias.

Side effects: marrow, mucositis, **secondary AML** (long latency, MLL gene rearrangement classic).

### Topoisomerase I inhibitors

| Drug | Use | Toxicity |
|---|---|---|
| **Irinotecan** | Colorectal | **Severe diarrhea** (acute - cholinergic - atropine; late - loperamide); UGT1A1 polymorphism affects metabolism |
| **Topotecan** | Ovarian, small cell lung | Marrow suppression |

## Checkpoint inhibitors (immunotherapy)

| Drug | Target | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab, Cemiplimab** | PD-1 | Many: melanoma, NSCLC, RCC, HNSCC, urothelial, MSI-high, etc. |
| **Atezolizumab, Durvalumab, Avelumab** | PD-L1 | NSCLC, urothelial, TNBC, ES-SCLC |
| **Ipilimumab** | CTLA-4 | Melanoma, RCC (with nivo) |
| **Relatlimab** | LAG-3 | Combined with nivo for melanoma |

**Side effects = immune-related adverse events (irAEs)**:
- Dermatitis (rash, pruritus, vitiligo).
- Colitis (any age - rule out C. diff, treat with steroids).
- Hepatitis.
- Pneumonitis.
- Endocrine: **hypophysitis** (esp. ipi), thyroid (hypo/hyper), **type 1 diabetes**, adrenal insufficiency.
- Nephritis.
- Myocarditis (rare but serious).

**Management of irAEs**: hold drug; high-dose **steroids** for grade 3-4; may need infliximab/MMF for steroid-refractory colitis.

## CAR-T cell therapy

| Product | Target | Indication |
|---|---|---|
| **Tisagenlecleucel** | CD19 | Pediatric ALL, large B-cell lymphoma |
| **Axicabtagene** | CD19 | LBCL, follicular |
| **Brexucabtagene** | CD19 | MCL, adult ALL |
| **Lisocabtagene** | CD19 | LBCL |
| **Idecabtagene, Ciltacabtagene** | BCMA | Multiple myeloma |

**Side effects**:
- **Cytokine release syndrome (CRS)** - fever, hypotension, hypoxia → **tocilizumab** (anti-IL-6R).
- **ICANS** (immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity) - confusion, aphasia, seizures → steroids.
- Cytopenias.

## L-asparaginase

Hydrolyzes serum asparagine (ALL cells can't synthesize). Used in ALL.

Side effects: **pancreatitis, hyperglycemia, hyperammonemia, thrombosis (depletes coag factors)**, hypersensitivity.

## Other notable drugs

- **Tretinoin (ATRA), Arsenic trioxide** - APL (M3 AML, t(15;17)). **Differentiation syndrome** - capillary leak, fever, hypoxia → dexamethasone.
- **Bortezomib, Carfilzomib, Ixazomib** - proteasome inhibitors; multiple myeloma. Neuropathy (bortezomib worst); cardiotoxicity (carfilzomib).
- **Lenalidomide, Pomalidomide, Thalidomide** - immunomodulators; MM. **Thalidomide teratogen**; VTE.

## Tumor lysis syndrome

Massive cell death (high tumor burden, sensitive tumor) → **K+, PO4, uric acid, LDH ↑, Ca ↓**, AKI, arrhythmia.

**Prevention/treatment**:
- IV hydration aggressive.
- **Allopurinol** or **rasburicase** (recombinant urate oxidase - very effective; **avoid G6PD** - hemolysis).
- Avoid potassium + phosphate in IVF.
- Dialysis if severe.

## Emesis prevention

| Risk | Antiemetic regimen |
|---|---|
| **High** (cisplatin, AC) | 5-HT3 (ondansetron) + NK1 (aprepitant) + dexamethasone ± olanzapine |
| **Moderate** (oxaliplatin, anthracyclines) | 5-HT3 + dexamethasone |
| **Low** | Dexamethasone or 5-HT3 |
| **Anticipatory** | Lorazepam |
| **Breakthrough** | Add metoclopramide, prochlorperazine, olanzapine |

<Clinical>
**Doxorubicin cardiotoxicity**: check baseline LVEF (echo or MUGA). Limit cumulative dose (~450-550 mg/m² ↑ risk). Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin reduces cardiac signal. Dexrazoxane for prevention in high-cumulative-dose patients.

**Cisplatin support**: aggressive IV hydration + osmotic diuresis. Pre-medicate with ondansetron + aprepitant + dexamethasone (highly emetogenic). Audiometry monitoring.

**Patient with febrile neutropenia post-chemo**: ANC &lt;500 + fever → empiric **cefepime** or **piperacillin-tazobactam** or carbapenem within 1 hour. Add vancomycin if line, mucositis, hypotension. G-CSF if anticipated prolonged.

**Trastuzumab-induced HF**: hold trastuzumab; start guideline HFrEF therapy; LVEF usually recovers. Can rechallenge once recovered.

**Bleomycin lung toxicity**: avoid high-FiO₂ general anesthesia (potentiates pulmonary toxicity). Bring up at preop consult.
</Clinical>
